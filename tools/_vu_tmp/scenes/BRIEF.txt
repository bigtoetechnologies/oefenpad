VASTE UITDRUKKINGEN — SCENE BRIEF
=================================

Recreated on 2026-11-20 from tools/_bw_tmp/scenes/BRIEF.txt (the original authoring
brief was lost) and adapted to fixed expressions (chunks). convert.py skips this file.

Each card in Docs/vaste-uitdrukkingen/ stands for one Dutch fixed expression (chunk).
The card is a picture brief: one drawing, in the same cobalt-on-white style as
Docs/voorzetselpaden/ and Docs/werkwoorden/, that makes the chunk obvious.
Chunks are learned as CONTRAST SETS: two to four chunks on one axis
(op tijd / te laat). The set is what teaches, so the drawings in a set must be the
SAME picture with ONE visible thing changed.

Read first (paths relative to the repo root):
  tools/_vu_tmp/clusters.txt             the sets, members, glosses and rank keys
  tools/_vu_tmp/left-out.tsv             chunks removed on purpose; do not bring them back
  Docs/werkwoorden/AGENTS.html           card conventions (what Must read / Avoid mean)
  Docs/voorzetselpaden/a2-pairs.html     how paired frames share a fixed setting

WHAT COUNTS AS A CHUNK
----------------------
- A chunk has a fixed form: no article (aan tafel, op zee), a frozen word (ter plekke,
  te voet) or a preposition the learner cannot predict (op het platteland).
- Not a chunk: free preposition-plus-noun combinations where any noun fits (in de trein,
  zonder melk, voor het raam). They belong to the single-preposition cards in Voorzetselpaden.
- Not a chunk: single words (naast, thuis, overdag, rechtop). They belong to Voorzetselpaden
  or Bijwoorden. A partner that another family already draws is named with
  '; xref=family:card-id' at the end of the cluster line, not drawn again.
- No card repeats a Voorzetselpaden chunk, example sentence or scene (boarding a vehicle is
  Voorzetselpaden stappen in / stappen uit; draw travel, not boarding). The checker tests chunks
  and example sentences against the Voorzetselpaden pages.

AUTHORING FORMAT (scenes/<group>.txt, '||' separators, one row per line)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
C||cluster||frame||sentence_nl||sentence_en
K||id||scene||must||avoid||contrast||example_nl||example_en||alt||register||literal

Then run, in this order:
  python3 tools/_vu_tmp/convert.py                   # .txt -> .clusters.tsv / .cards.tsv
  python3 tools/_vu_tmp/select.py                    # clusters.txt -> selection.json
  python3 tools/build-vaste-uitdrukkingen-cards.py   # pages
  python3 tools/check-vaste-uitdrukkingen-cards.py   # must report 0 errors

Fields
  cluster      the cluster id, exactly as in clusters.txt
  frame        English. The FIXED part of every drawing in the set: same figure(s) or object,
               same plain setting, same viewpoint, same size on the page. Then one final
               sentence starting "Only ..." that names the single thing that changes.
  sentence_nl  Dutch. ONE contrast sentence that uses all chunks of the set. Plain grammar.
  sentence_en  English translation.
  id           the member id from clusters.txt; it is also the png name.
  scene        English, 25-75 words (the checker enforces it). Starts from the frame and states
               the changed element. Name every object that must be visible. Any board, screen,
               book or page in a scene is stated to be blank.
  must         English. The one mark that must read at thumbnail size. One clause.
  avoid        English. Three to five near misses separated by semicolons, each with the competing
               chunk in brackets. The first item names a partner (or the cross-referenced card).
  contrast     English. Starts "Pair with <partner ids>." Then ONE sentence naming the single point
               of difference.
  example_nl   Dutch. One natural sentence with the chunk in the sense drawn. Not the set sentence,
               and not a sentence Voorzetselpaden already uses.
  example_en   English translation.
  alt          Dutch. One sentence for a screen reader that describes the picture. It never
               contains the chunk, a form of it (any article or possessive in between), a partner
               chunk, or the stem of a content word that only that member has (linkerkant ->
               no "linker...", 's ochtends -> no "ochtend..."). A noun shared by the whole set
               (tafel in aan / op / onder tafel) may be named.
  register     neutral, spoken or formal. 's ochtends, 's avonds, met zijn tweeën and met zijn allen
               are standard written Dutch: neutral. spoken is for chunks like in zijn eentje.
  literal      literal, or "literal and figurative" when a common second sense exists
               (hand in hand gaan met, alles op een rij zetten). Temporal or grammatical chunks
               with one sense (voor het eerst, in plaats van) are literal.

HARD RULES
----------
- Never colour as the carrier of meaning: nothing is "red" or "yellow". Light is drawn as a
  lamp with rays or a sun, dark as hatching.
- No text, numbers or writing inside the drawing: no signs, no clock faces, no sleep "Zzz"
  bubbles, no arrows. Direction is shown by the way a body faces and which foot leads.
- No souvenir Dutch imagery (windmills, clogs, tulips, canals, cheese).
- Faces: emotions are shown with brows, eyes and mouth on a white outlined face; keep the same
  face across a set unless the face is the thing that changes.
- A chunk that cannot be shown in one picture (it needs a sequence, a sign or motion) is written
  with scene "SKIP: <reason>" and the other fields "-"; the builder lists it on the overview.
  Skip a set as a whole, never one half, so no drawn card loses its partner.
- One sense per card. Do not use real brands, real people or religious symbols.
- Dutch must be correct. If you are unsure of a form, choose a sentence that avoids the problem.
- No tab character inside any field; no line breaks inside a field.
