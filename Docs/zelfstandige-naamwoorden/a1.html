de stoel · de bank

kind of seat · 2 cards

De stoel is voor één persoon, maar de bank is voor drie personen.The chair is for one person, but the sofa is for three people.

Fixed frame. one seat seen three-quarter from the front on a flat floor line against a plain wall, centred, same size on the page, no person, no cushion pattern. Only the kind of seat changes: a single seat with a back, or a wide seat for several people.

#12
geen beeld

de stoel

chair

destoelen

kind of seatA1

Scene
A single plain wooden chair with four legs, a flat seat and an upright back, seen three-quarter from the front on the floor line against a plain wall. It is narrow: the seat is about the width of one person's hips. No cushion, no arms.
Must read
a narrow seat with four legs and an upright back, wide enough for one person.
Avoid
a wide seat with a cushioned back and arms for several people (bank); a stool with no back (not the card); an armchair with thick padding (bank); a folding deckchair (not the card).
Contrast
Pair with bank. The only difference is the seat: narrow and hard with an upright back here, wide and padded for several people there.
Example
Er staat een stoel bij de tafel.
There is a chair by the table.
Alt text
Een smal houten zitje met vier poten en een rechte rugleuning staat tegen een lege muur.
