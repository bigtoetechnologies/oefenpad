het huis · de flat

kind of home · 2 cards

Het gezin woont in het huis, maar de student woont in de flat.The family lives in the house, but the student lives in the flat.

Fixed frame. one whole building seen from the front on a flat ground line, centred, same size on the page, open sky with no clouds, no people. Only the kind of building changes: a house with its own roof and door, or a tall block of stacked homes.

#1
geen beeld

het huis

house

hethuizen

kind of homeA0

Scene
A single detached two-storey house with a pitched roof, a chimney, one front door at ground level and two windows upstairs, standing alone on the ground line. Nothing is stacked on top of it and no neighbour touches it. The huis-een and huis-meer cards reuse this drawing.
Must read
one house with its own pitched roof and its own front door at ground level.
Avoid
a tall block with many identical floors (flat); a row of joined houses sharing walls (not the card); a shed or hut with no windows (not a home); a castle with towers (not the card).
Contrast
Pair with flat. The only difference is the building: one low house with its own door and roof here, a tall block of many identical floors there.
Example
Wij wonen in een huis met een tuin.
We live in a house with a garden.
Alt text
Een laag gebouw met een schuin dak, een schoorsteen en een deur aan de voorkant.

kind of homeA0

Scene
A tall rectangular block with six identical floors of windows, a small balcony under each upper window, a flat roof and one shared entrance door at ground level, standing alone on the ground line. No people, no sign, no neighbouring building.
Must read
one tall block of stacked identical floors with balconies and a flat roof.

a single low house with a pitched roof (huis); a row of terraced houses (huis); an office tower with no balconies (not the card); a hotel with a sign (needs text).

Example

Een hoog gebouw met zes verdiepingen, kleine balkons en een plat dak.

de auto · de bus · de fiets

kind of vehicle · 3 cards

Ik ga met de auto, met de bus of met de fiets.I go by car, by bus or by bicycle.

Fixed frame. one vehicle seen side-on, whole object, wheels touching a flat road line, centred, same height on the page, a plain low wall behind, no driver or passengers visible. Only the kind of vehicle changes: a car, a bus or a bicycle.

#5
geen beeld

de auto

car

deauto's

kind of vehicleA0

Scene
A small passenger car seen side-on, two of four wheels visible, two side doors, a sloping windscreen, a roof and a short boot, wheels on a flat road line, no driver. Two small side windows show empty seats; the body is short and boxy.
Must read
a short closed car body with doors and two side windows on four wheels.
Avoid
a long vehicle with a row of six windows (bus); a bicycle with two thin spoked wheels (fiets); a lorry with a box on the back (not the card); a train (not the card).
Contrast
Pair with bus and fiets. The only difference is the vehicle: a short body with two windows here, a long body with a row of windows or two thin wheels there.
Example
Mijn vader heeft een nieuwe auto.
My father has a new car.
Alt text
Een korte gesloten wagen met twee deuren staat op een rechte weg.

Fixed frame. a wide view of a settlement from a gentle rise on the ground line, same viewpoint, plain sky, one road leading in from the front edge of the picture. Only the size of the settlement changes: a dense city of tall blocks, or a few low houses around a small square.

Scene
A wide view of a city from a gentle rise: about ten tall blocks of many floors standing close together, one high tower among them and one road leading in from the front with three small cars on it. Rows of identical windows on each block.

tall blocks packed close together with a tower and one road.

het huis · de huizen

one or more · 2 cards

Hier staat het huis, daar staan de huizen.Here stands the house, over there stand the houses.

Fixed frame. a detached two-storey house with a pitched roof, a chimney, one door and two windows (the huis card drawing) on a flat ground line, every house identical in size and style, plain sky, same viewpoint. Only the number of houses changes: one house, or three houses side by side with gaps between them.

Scene
The same drawing as the huis card: one detached two-storey house with a pitched roof, a chimney, one front door and two windows, in the middle of the picture on a flat ground line under plain sky, with empty ground on both sides.
Must read
one single house with empty ground on both sides.
Avoid
three houses in a row (huizen); a terrace of joined houses (not the card); a tall block (flat); a shed (not the card).
Contrast
Pair with huis-meer. The only difference is the number: one house here, three identical houses side by side there.
Example
Het huis heeft een hoge deur.
The house has a tall door.
Alt text
Een enkel gebouw met een schuin dak en een schoorsteen staat in het midden op een vlakke grond.

Three copies of the huis drawing: identical detached two-storey houses with pitched roofs, chimneys, one front door and two windows each, evenly spaced with gaps of empty ground between them on one flat ground line under plain sky, each the same size as the single house.

three identical houses separated by gaps.
