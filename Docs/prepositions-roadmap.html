Voorzetselpaden — curriculum roadmap

The plan to 710 frames, and what B2 means.

Status: planning document, 9 November 2026. This extends the pilot spec in prepositions.html from the thirty-frame pilot to a 700-frame curriculum. It changes no shipped code and settles no product decision on its own. Where this file and prepositions.html disagree about the game as built, prepositions.html and prepositions-implementation.html win.

Two things it does decide:

  1. The count. 700 is the receptive ceiling — the number of chunks a learner should recognise and understand at B2. The productive target is smaller: roughly 250–400 chunks actually produced in speech.
  2. The shape. The first ~400 frames get the full four-stop loop. The last ~300 are receptive-first and may ship without Bouw de zin.

The numbers are estimates. The repo contains no frequency list and no CEFR chunk count; CEFR does not define one either. Order them by frequency before trusting the batch boundaries.

1. Two different counts

Do not conflate the closed class with the chunks built on it.

What is counted Dutch total What it means
Preposition words ~25 core, ~40–45 including formal aan, achter, bij, binnen, boven, buiten, door, in, langs, met, na, naar, naast, om, onder, op, over, rond, sinds, tegen, tussen, uit, van, vanaf, voor, zonder plus tijdens, volgens, wegens, ondanks, behalve, vanwege, via, richting, per, gezien, aangaande, betreffende, jegens, overeenkomstig
Verb / adjective / noun + preposition frames 1,000+ fixed combinations beschikken over, trots op, behoefte aan. The game's unit

The shipped A0–A2 set already uses 13 of the core prepositions across its 50 frames: aan, bij, door, in, met, naar, om, op, over, tegen, uit, van, voor. So the word inventory is not the work. The chunk inventory is.

2. What the numbers mean

Target Productive (speaking) Receptive (reading, listening)
A2 survival kit ~40–50 ~80
B1 ~100–150 ~250
B2 ~250–400 ~500–700

The original concept (archive/Concept.html) committed only to 30–50 chunks for the survival-kit pillar. That is an A2 target, not a B2 one. The 700 in this file is the top of the B2 receptive band; 500 is the floor.

The reconciliation with the batch plan below:

  • Phases A–C build about 400 frames with the full loop, meaning and form both scored. That covers the productive B2 band (250–400).
  • Phases D–E add about 300 more, receptive-first. They extend what the learner understands, not what they must produce.

3. Where the pilot sits

Shipped
Frames 50
Levels 33 (twelve pairs, two sets of three, nineteen singles)
Prepositions governed as frames 13
Scenes 50 raster masks
CEFR band A0–A2

Batch 4b (Docs/voorzetselpaden/a2-pairs.html) is drawn but not yet integrated. When it ships it takes the set to 66 frames, 36 levels, 23 pairs, three sets of three, ten singles and 13 prepositions, as recorded in that file.

The shipped 50 are concentrated at the bottom of the ladder. They are roughly 7% of the 710 target and about 12% of the productive B2 band. The remaining build is 660 frames, not ~120.

29 new batches, 680 new frames, 710 in total. With batch 4b that becomes 30 new batches, 696 new frames and 726 in total. At the current maximum of three frames per level that is roughly 250–300 levels.

Batch 8 was planned as pronominal adverbs plus separable-prefix frames. A separable verb governs no preposition, so the 29 separable frames drafted for it and for batches 9–13 were cut on 10 November 2026 and the prefix material moved to the Uit Elkaar game idea in uit-elkaar.html. Batch 8 keeps the pronominal adverbs; the freed slots went to high-frequency everyday chunks the set was missing (hopen op, uitgeven aan, bellen met, …). Three of the originally drafted chunks — gaan naar, kiezen voor and betalen voor — are also drawn in batch 4b. Each of the three is drawn in a different scene in each band, so only the chunk repeats; kiezen voor (cake over an apple in 4b) cannot be mistaken for its A2+ partner, while the other two differ only in place or object. One of three resolutions must be chosen before A2+-08 is drawn: keep both scenes and put each chunk in a level apart from its 4b partner (no new art); drop the three A2+ rows and plan three replacements from the B1 and B1+ chunks (band stays at 80); or point the three rows at the 4b art (they become documentation, not frames). Until then the three rows stay planned. This text moved here from the A2+ page on 12 Nov 2026, when the old Markdown prose was taken off the band pages. tussen was going to move from batch 5 to 4b; it stays in batch 5 now that kiezen tussen has been dropped from 4b.

Batch 32 exists because phases D and E named nine prepositions that no frame ever used. It is a coverage close-out, not new curriculum.

Batch 4b adds 16 frames at A2. The Σ column below batch 4 is therefore 16 higher than the table shows: batch 5 is 86, batch 8 is 146, batch 13 is 266, batch 19 is 416, batch 25 is 566 and the close-out is 726, not 710. Δ values are unchanged. The extra 16 come out of the same A2 budget, so the 710 in this file's title is now a floor rather than a ceiling.

The image plans were split into six pages on 10 November 2026 so that each step is smaller, and are now HTML pages in Docs/voorzetselpaden/: a0-a2.html (batch 4, with the 30 pilot frames), a2-pairs.html (4b), a2-plus.html (5–8, 80 frames), b1.html (9–13, 120 frames), b1-plus.html (14–23, 250 frames) and b2.html (24–32, 210 frames). index.html there is the overview. The batch numbers in this roadmap are unchanged; only the Band column and the page names follow the split. The Band values map to the files one-to-one: A2 → a0-a2 and a2-pairs, A2+ → a2-plus, B1 → b1, B1+ → b1-plus, B2 → b2.

The last row is batch 32 and is the one category that is not a chunk. These words govern nothing, so the only way to teach them is the situation they turn up in; §11 excludes free use everywhere else.

The register batches (19, 24–31) are a cross-cut: they draw from the categories above rather than adding a seventh category. Do not count them twice.

The phase rows above say where a preposition is introduced in the plan. An audit on 10 November 2026 found that nine of them had no frame anywhere in the art plans, zonder included, and that via appears only inside verspreiden via. Batch 32 frames the nine. via is still single-sourced.

8. What changes in the engine

The game now ships 91 example scenes across 28 levels (A0 and A1). 700 needs these changes. None of them are in prepositions-implementation.html yet.

Change Why
Split prepositions.json by phase or batch A 700-frame single file is unreviewable and slow to load. One file per phase, merged into the server-side GameContent catalog at load
Add band (A2/A2+/B1/B1+/B2/B2+) to levels Lets the UI order and gate content without hard-coded level arrays
Add register (everyday/work/academic/legal/medical/colloquial/literary) to frames A chunk can be correct and still wrong for the situation; the feedback should say so
Make Bouw conditional, like Klopt Receptive-only frames have no accepted-sentence list. Skip Bouw when bouw is absent instead of rendering an empty step
Add a sense key where a frame string repeats werken aan (a project) and werken bij (an employer) should not collide; two frames may share a label but not an id
Give receptive-only frames a second exercise type Kies is one item per frame. A 25-frame batch with no contrast and no Bouw is 25 taps and no feedback loop. Either pair them or add a meaning-matching mode
Build Krimp as a stop Pronominal adverbs are 40 frames and a core B2 skill. Today the form is a Korter: line on Ontdek
Persist misses by frame id 700 frames without a review queue is a dictionary. The mistakes queue in game-catalogue.html is the feature

contrast stays the only source of a false item. Do not add random distractors to make the later batches work; a random wrong preposition teaches nothing, per prepositions.html.

9. What happens to the art

710 scenes is not a plan; it is a decade. The pilot already uses raster, which game-art.html calls a placeholder. For this volume:

All six slices are planned, as pages in Docs/voorzetselpaden/: a0-a2.html has one card for each of the twenty batch-4 frames and two copy-paste Grok prompts, a2-pairs.html one card for each of the sixteen batch-4b frames, a2-plus.html the 80 A2+ frames, b1.html the 120 B1 frames, b1-plus.html the 250 B1+ frames and b2.html the 210 B2 frames.

Phase Frames New scenes (est.) Strategy
A 100 50 One scene per contrast pair, plus one per distinct situation
B 120 50 Reuse everyday scenes; add only where the situation is new
C 150 50 Register scenes reused across chunks (an office, a counter, a clinic)
D 150 35 Register-generic scenes; abstract or symbolic for formal chunks
E 150 30 Reuse the register set; only exceptions get new art
Close-out 10 0 Reuse the register set; bare prepositions need no new scene
Total 680 new ~215 new (≈245 with pilot)

Three consequences:

  • Return to SVG for all new art. Raster was a pilot decision; game-art.html already says new art starts as SVG. Hundreds of redraws in raster is not maintainable.
  • Scene reuse becomes a data rule, not an accident. A scene may serve several frames; the data model already allows it (scenes is separate from frames).
  • The meaning-first promise weakens in phases D–E. A formal chunk like aangaande has no honest silhouette. Flag those as gloss-and-context frames rather than pretending a picture carries the meaning.

10. Session, study time and assessment

  • A two-frame level is 8 scored answers, roughly six minutes. 250–300 levels is about 25–30 hours of first-pass play, before review.
  • At 5–10 new chunks a day, 700 chunks is 70–140 days of study. That is a realistic B2 timeline for an adult learner, not a content defect.
  • This game does not assess speaking. It trains recognition and written production. B2 speaking also needs audio, pronunciation and free output, which game-catalogue.html lists as gaps. Do not claim the 700 closes them.

11. What the 700 does not include

  • Free spatial and temporal use (op tafel, in de kast, na de les). That is grammar and vocabulary, not chunk learning.
  • Preposition stranding and word order beyond the frame.
  • Audio, pronunciation or listening.
  • Free speaking or interaction.
  • Every fixed combination in Dutch. The universe is 1,000+; 700 is a B2 slice.

12. Sequencing rules

  1. Frequency before completeness. Order every batch by corpus frequency, not by alphabetical neatness. If a frequency list arrives, re-cut the batches around it.
  2. Contrast before single. A pair teaches meaning; a single teaches form. Spend the extra art and data on pairs.
  3. One new preposition per level. Do not introduce two new prepositions and a new verb in the same level.
  4. Recycle older frames as options. Later batches should draw distractors from earlier prepositions, not only from the immediate contrast partner, or the learner learns the pair and nothing else.
  5. Register last. A learner who cannot produce wachten op does not need aangaande.

13. Open questions

  • Product scope. game-catalogue.html states the target level as A1 → B1. This roadmap runs to B2. That is a product decision for the owner, not a doc edit: does Voorzetselpaden own B2, or does it stop at B1 and leave B2 to reading and speaking work?
  • Ship all 700, or build to 400 and stop? Phases D–E are receptive and expensive to vet. The productive B2 band is complete at the end of phase C.
  • Where does the frequency order come from? No list exists in the repo. SUBTLEX-NL or an NT2 lemma list would settle the batch boundaries.
  • Do receptive-only levels need a new exercise type? Kies alone is too thin, and a single frame has no Klopt.
  • Does the mistakes queue gate release? Without it, 700 frames is browsing, not learning.
  • Art budget. ~215 new scenes is still large. Which phases may use one scene across several chunks, and who signs that off?
