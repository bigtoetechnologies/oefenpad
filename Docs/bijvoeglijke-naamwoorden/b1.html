#101

straightnessB1

Scene
one plain wooden stick lies across the full width of the page on a plain flat ground line, side view, both ends touching two small stones. The stick lies perfectly level, and a thin straight guide line is drawn just above it, parallel and equal in length, so no part of the stick bends away.

Fixed frame. one woman sitting on a plain stool, seen from the front, whole figure, same size and place on the page, playing a guitar held across her lap, plain wall and floor. Only the guitar and what it is connected to change.

akoestisch

acoustic

kind of guitarB1

Scene
one woman on a plain stool, front view, whole figure, plain wall and floor. Across her lap she plays a guitar with a deep rounded body and a round sound hole under the strings. No cable and no amplifier; three small curved sound lines rise from the sound hole.
Must read
the round sound hole in a deep guitar body, with no cable.
Avoid
a flat solid guitar with a cable to an amplifier (elektrisch); a tiny ukulele (klein); the woman singing with a wide-open mouth (luid); a guitar with a snapped string (kapot).
Contrast
Pair with elektrisch. The only difference is the guitar: a deep body with a sound hole and no cable here, a flat solid body with a cable to an amplifier there.
Example
Hij speelt akoestische muziek in het café.
He plays acoustic music in the café.
Alt text
Een vrouw speelt gitaar; in de bolle klankkast zit een rond gat en er hangt geen snoer aan.

Must read
a cable from the flat guitar to an amplifier box on the floor.
Avoid
a deep guitar with a round sound hole and no cable (akoestisch); a microphone on a stand (luid); a guitar with a snapped string (kapot); a lamp with a cord and rays (licht).
Contrast
Pair with akoestisch. The only difference is the guitar: a flat solid body with a cable to an amplifier here, a deep body with a sound hole and no cable there.

Fixed frame. one plain short-sleeved T-shirt laid flat on a plain table, seen from straight above, whole shirt, same size, outline and place on the page in every drawing, no logo, no letters. Only the pattern on the fabric changes.

Scene
one plain short-sleeved T-shirt laid flat on a plain table, seen from straight above, whole shirt. Seven straight lines of even width run across the front and the sleeves, evenly spaced from collar to hem and all parallel. No other marks, no logo, no letters.
Must read
evenly spaced straight lines across the whole shirt.
Avoid
a grid of crossing lines (geruit); round dots spread over the shirt (gestippeld); a plain shirt without marks (effen); a shirt with crease lines (gekreukt).
Contrast
Pair with geruit, gestippeld. The only difference is the pattern: parallel lines here, a grid of squares or round dots there.
