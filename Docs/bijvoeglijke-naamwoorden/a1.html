age of a person · 2 cards

Fixed frame. One person standing alone, whole figure, front view, same size on the page, on a plain ground line with a plain low wall and a small bench behind. Same simple clothes. Only the age of the person changes: body size, hair and face lines.

#15
geen beeld

jong

young

age of a personA1

Scene
one child about seven years old stands alone on a ground line beside a small bench, front view, whole figure. The body is small and the head is large for it, the face is smooth and round, the hair is short and full. A small ball lies at the feet.
Must read
a small child with a smooth round face and a ball at the feet.
Avoid
a bent old person with thin hair and a walking stick (oud-mensen); a small adult beside a big adult (klein); a baby lying in a cot (not the card); a child asleep on the bench (not the card).
Contrast
Pair with oud-mensen. The only difference is age: a small child with a smooth face and a ball here, a bent man with thin hair and a stick there.
Example

Alt text
