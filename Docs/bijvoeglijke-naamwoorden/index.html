How the cards work

Adjectives are taught in contrast sets: two or three words on one axis (groot/klein, heet/warm/koud). Every drawing in a set is the same picture with one visible thing changed, so the learner sees the pair, not the word.

A card carries the adjective, an English gloss, the scene brief, the one mark that must read, the near misses, the contrast note naming its partner and the single point of difference, an example sentence, and the alt text. Each set also carries a fixed frame and one Dutch sentence using every member.

How a set was placed

A set sits in the band of its most frequent member (frequency rank in tools/lexicon/woorden-frequentie.tsv: A0 up to 250, A1 251–1000, A2 1001–2500, B1 2501–5000, B2 5001–10000): the learner already knows one half and the card teaches its partner. Ranks are looked up by exact lemma. Homograph split rows such as hol (adj) are ranked after the real list, so their number is not a frequency and is not used.

Homograph ranks were checked by hand: laat (verb laten), vroeg (verb vragen) and heel (adverb) overstate frequency; the time words stay in A0 by rank, and heel/kapot is banded by kapot. Everyday sets promoted by judgment

Everyday items sit at the lowest sensible level, A2 at the highest (owner rule, 20 Nov 2026). Where frequency rank put a set of daily home, food or shopping words in B1 or B2, the whole set moved to A2. Left out on purpose

  • Colours (rood, blauw, groen ...): the art style is one cobalt on white, so colour cannot carry meaning.
  • Abstract adjectives (belangrijk, mogelijk, nodig ...): no honest single picture; they follow the abstract-verb rule.
  • Dutch cliché food (chips, cheese, herring): souvenir imagery, so knapperig/slap is drawn as a bowl of lettuce, not a paper cone of chips.

Cards written up as SKIP

  • rijp (B1, rijpheid): ripeness is carried by colour, which the cobalt-only style excludes; drawn by size it reads groot/klein (review 20 Nov 2026, H9).
  • onrijp (B1, rijpheid): ripeness is carried by colour, which the cobalt-only style excludes; drawn by size it reads groot/klein (review 20 Nov 2026, H9).
  • geschild (B2, schil): duplicate of werkwoorden:schillen, which draws the same apple with one long curl of peel; Werkwoorden has priority.
  • ongeschild (B2, schil): duplicate of werkwoorden:schillen, which draws the same apple with one long curl of peel; Werkwoorden has priority.

Words shared with other families

Where two families card the same item or scene, the higher family keeps it (Werkwoorden, Voorzetselpaden, Bijvoeglijke naamwoorden, Zelfstandige naamwoorden, Bijwoorden, Scheidbare werkwoorden, Vaste uitdrukkingen; owner ruling, 20 Nov 2026). This family keeps opgepompt/leeggelopen, so Scheidbare werkwoorden drops oppompen/leeglopen; it keeps horizontaal/verticaal and opgerold, so Bijwoorden drops them. It drops geschild/ongeschild, which repeats Werkwoorden schillen.

Why B1 and B2 have fewer than 20 new sets

The brief asked for at least 20 new contrast sets in each of B1 and B2. The 20 Nov 2026 expansion added 15 to B1 (5 of them since promoted to A2 as everyday sets) and 14 to B2; the candidates below were checked and dropped.
