groot · klein

size · 2 cards

Deze kist is groot, maar die kist is klein.This crate is big, but that crate is small.

Fixed frame. one adult in plain clothes stands whole-figure, side view, on a flat ground line beside a single plain wooden crate with a lid, same distance from the viewer, a plain low wall behind. Only the size of the crate changes.

#1
geen beeld

groot

big

sizeA0

Scene
one adult in plain clothes stands whole-figure, side view, beside one huge plain wooden crate with a lid whose top rises far above the adult's head and whose width is more than twice the shoulders. The adult looks small beside it and the wall behind is plain.
Must read
a crate towering far above the person's head.
Avoid
a crate the size of a shoe (klein); a tall thin shape (hoog); a long low box (lang); a heavy crate with a person bending under it (zwaar).
Contrast
Pair with klein. The only difference is the crate: far above the person's head here, no higher than the person's shoe there.
Example
Ons nieuwe huis is erg groot.
Our new house is very big.
Alt text
Een man staat naast een enorme kist die veel hoger is dan zijn hoofd.

Fixed frame. one adult on a plain bicycle in a long plain street, side view, whole figure, same distance from the viewer, a plain low wall behind and a flat ground line. Only the pace of the cyclist changes.

#9

speedA0

Scene
one adult on a plain bicycle seen side-on, leaning forward, legs mid-turn, three bold speed lines trailing behind the bike and the wheels drawn slightly apart from the ground line. A small stone marker well behind the bike shows how far they have come from the start line.
Must read

Fixed frame. one plain vase, intact, holding one flower that is not a tulip, stands centred on a plain table, front view, same size on the page, a plain wall behind; one adult at the right, head and shoulders, looks at it. Only the pattern on the vase and the onlooker's face change.

#11
geen beeld

mooi

beautiful

looks of a thingA0

Scene
one plain vase holding one flower with a straight stem and a round head of petals, not a tulip, stands centred on a plain table, front view. The vase is intact and smooth with an even, regular swirl pattern. One adult at the right, head and shoulders, looks at it with raised brows and a soft smile.
Must read
an even swirl on the vase and an admiring face beside it.
Avoid
the same vase with clashing blotches and a grimacing onlooker (lelijk); a vase with a large bow (not the card); a tidy empty vase (leeg); a vase broken in pieces (kapot).
Contrast
Pair with lelijk. The only difference is the pattern and the onlooker: an even swirl and an admiring face here, clashing blotches and a grimace there.
Example

Alt text

Fixed frame. one adult in plain clothes with a bag at a plain bus stop, whole figure, side view, a post with a blank sign plate, a plain road, a flat ground line and one plain bus on the road. Only where the bus is and what the adult does change.

#13
geen beeld

vroeg

time of dayA0

Scene
one adult in plain clothes with a bag stands calmly at a plain bus stop beside a post with a blank sign plate, whole figure, side view. Far down the plain road at the left, one plain bus is still approaching, drawn small. The adult waits, relaxed, looking toward it. No sun, moon, clock or numerals.
Must read
the adult waiting while the bus is still far away and coming.
Avoid

Contrast
Pair with laat. The only difference is the bus and the adult: the bus still coming and the adult waiting here, the bus driving off and the adult running after it there.
Example

Alt text
Een man met een tas wacht bij een bushalte; de bus is nog ver weg en komt eraan.
