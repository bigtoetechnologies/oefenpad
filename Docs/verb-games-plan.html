Verb games — the prompt series, the gaps and the plan

A review of the verb-content prompt series, the questions it raises, and a small-step plan for building the verb games.

Docs/verb-games-plan.html

On this page
  • The verdict: it builds the wrong thing first
  • What the repo already settles
  • The series, phase by phase
  • Questions to settle first
  • Draft plan, in small steps
  • Defaults and housekeeping

Status: review and open questions, 19 November 2026. This page reviews the series of prompts in Docs/plans/verb_generation_teaching_prompts.md — the plan for generating Dutch verb content and adding verb mini-games — and lists what has to be decided before any of it is built. Nothing on this page is agreed yet.

The series was written without the rest of this documentation in view. Three of its assumptions contradict decisions that are already settled, and each fix changes the shape of the work rather than the wording of it.

The verdict: it builds the wrong thing first

  1. The verb game the repo already recommends is not in the series. game-catalogue.html names verb conjugation (Pillar 6) as the biggest gap in the set and its candidate as Werkwoordtrainer: infinitive + subject + tense → produce the form, with a strong-verb prediction round (lopen → liep → gelopen) and a separable level. The prompts instead bolt "verb" onto Galgje, Woordzoeker and Geheugen. Those are vocabulary games; a conjugated form in a grid teaches the spelling of that form, not that ik liep exists. If both are built, Werkwoordtrainer is the one that fills the pillar.
  2. Phase 4 duplicates Uit Elkaar, which already specifies the separable-verbs game — and specifies it as text tiles with no art: “the unit is a sentence with a moving part … art is polish rather than a dependency”. Prompt 4.1 asks for four image prompts per separable verb. One of the two documents has to give way.
  3. The scale is off by an order of magnitude. Four scenes per verb across the 1,638 verbs in the lexicon is roughly 6,500 images. The site has 117 preposition scenes and 148 word images, and 271 preposition cards still have no art (project rules, “Where things stand”). Verb art needs its own budget decision, not a line in a schema.

Mechanically the series also cannot be run as written: it writes .md files into Docs/, and the project rules forbid that — the HTML is the document. The prompt file itself is the only .md left under Docs/.

What the repo already settles

Constraint Where it is settled What it forces
The verb game is form production, not spelling game-catalogue.html, “Verb conjugation — Pillar 6 is unserved” Werkwoordtrainer first. Verb categories in Galgje and Woordzoeker are a cheap extra, not the answer.
Separable verbs are a text game uit-elkaar.html Phase 4’s image prompts are redundant. Reuse the modes and the 23-verb list already handed over.
1,638 verbs in the lexicon, lemma and gloss only tools/lexicon/woorden-woordsoort.tsv Conjugations must be authored or imported. Nothing in the repo can be derived into a verb table. Update, 20 November 2026: the irregular verb cards in Docs/werkwoorden/ now carry the past singular and perfect, imported from Bijlage 3b into tools/lexicon/werkwoorden-vormen.tsv (104 verbs, A0 to B1). Regular verbs, other persons and other tenses are still unsourced.
Galgje’s alphabet is A–Z server/Oefenpad.Services/Games/HangmanService.cs No IJ as one letter, no accents, no spaces. A form such as heb gelopen cannot be guessed; opgebeld can.
Woordzoeker places A–Z single tokens WordSearchService.cs Multi-word forms are out. opgebeld is in, bel op is not.
Geheugen and Koppelen are word ↔ picture decks MemoryService.cs, MatchingService.cs A verb deck needs one picture per verb. Stem ↔ participle is a different, meaning-free drill.
Rounds are generated server-side from JSON games-backend.html Verb data is a game resource; the browsable master can still be HTML that generates it.
Docs are HTML and never .md project-rules.html The spec, the batch files and this review all need a home that is not plans/*.md.
117 scenes, 148 word images, 271 unarted cards project-rules.html Art is the bottleneck and the review queue, exactly as it already is for prepositions.

The series, phase by phase

Phase What the series produces Verdict
1 — Irregular verbs A schema, two to four batch files and one master; up to four sentences and four image prompts per verb Right first audience, wrong unit. Keep the verb list; cut the per-sentence art until one verb has been proved end to end.
2 — Regular verbs The same again from the lexicon, “matching voorzetselpaden” “Matching Voorzetselpaden” can only mean the level spine (Ontdek → oefen → bouw). A regular verb governs no fixed preposition, so the frame-and-contrast machinery does not apply and this needs its own spec.
3 — Teaching formats Hangman, Word Search and a Memory variant, plus two or three more formats Galgje and Woordzoeker can take verbs as data. The memory variant carries no meaning (question 9). Werkwoordtrainer is missing from the list.
4 — Separable verbs A spec, batches and a master, with image prompts Duplicates uit-elkaar.html, and the art contradicts that document’s own decision.

Questions to settle first

Scope and shape

  1. What is the deliverable: a new Werkwoordtrainer game, verb categories inside the existing games, or both — and in what order?
  2. Which verbs, which bands? (a) the sixty to eighty highest-frequency irregulars at A1–A2, (b) all ~200 irregulars, (c) the top N regulars out of the 1,638 in woorden-woordsoort.tsv. Option (a) is the inburgering audience and is reviewable in one sitting.
  3. Which tenses per band? Proposed: A1 presens and perfectum; A2 imperfectum, modal and infinitive; B1 plusquamperfectum, passive and subclause order. Is futurum (zal lopen) wanted at all? It is low value at A2.

Art

  1. Is the picture per verb — one scene, reused — or per sentence? Per sentence is what makes Voorzetselpaden work, but it is four times the art, and the sentence rather than the verb becomes the unit.
  2. Who draws them, and do they follow game-art.html (512 px, cobalt, no text inside the artwork)? Is there a “proposal, no art yet” state, as for the 271 preposition cards, so a verb game can ship text-first?

Data and format

  1. Where do the master files live? (a) HTML pages under Docs/werkwoorden/ as the master, with an import script producing verbs.json — the Voorzetselpaden pipeline, which keeps the rule and is what made review possible; or (b) JSON as the master, with no browsable documentation.
  2. Do example sentences carry an English gloss? The rule is English-free, with glosses as a convenience only. If feedback relies on the gloss, the game fails the rule.

Inside the existing games

  1. What is the Galgje clue? A tense hint is Dutch text the learner has to parse. A picture, an infinitive or a gloss each change what the round teaches.
  2. Geheugen: stem ↔ conjugated form carries no meaning and pairs things that are not equivalents (loop ↔ gelopen). Keep it, or pair infinitive ↔ picture and leave form work to Werkwoordtrainer?

Verification and gating

  1. Partly answered, 20 November 2026: unpredictable forms count as vocabulary, so irregular cards carry the past singular and perfect from a checked source (Bijlage 3b). The question below still applies to everything else. Conjugations have to be checked, not generated. The repo has no inflection source — the lexicon is lemma and gloss, and kaikki supplied the lemmas it did, though its Dutch entries do carry inflections under CC BY-SA. Checked source, or hand-authoring with a review pass like the 13 November preposition audit?
  2. Do verb items need CEFR bands and theme tags (Boodschappen, Mensen)? The 16 November gate cannot be built without theme tags, and Voorzetselpaden has none. If verbs become the first themed content, the schema changes.

Draft plan, in small steps

Each step is independently shippable and reviewable, and none of them depends on a large generation run.

# Step Output Why this order
0 Answer questions 1–4 A one-page decision note Everything except these four is cheap to change later
1 A ten-verb pilot: irregulars, hand-written, one screen Docs/werkwoorden/ page plus verbs.json Proves the schema, the level spine and the review format on ten items instead of two hundred
2 Import script, service and route Werkwoordtrainer playable with ten verbs Same shape as tools/import-prepositions-band.py; the game exists before the content does
3 Grow to about sixty verbs, batch by batch Batches merged into the master Only once step 1 has survived review
4 Art pass for the pilot only Ten scenes or fewer Decides per-verb versus per-sentence with evidence rather than argument
5 A verb category in Galgje and Woordzoeker Word lists only Cheapest win, once questions 8 and 9 are answered
6 Separable verbs Follows uit-elkaar.html, text only Reconcile with Phase 4 of the prompt series first

Defaults and housekeeping

Two defaults unless someone objects: verbs.json under server/Oefenpad.Resources/Games/Data is always generated and never hand-edited, and the HTML page is the master — the rule already used for prepositions (Working notes for agents).

Housekeeping: Docs/plans/verb_generation_teaching_prompts.md should become this page, or be deleted. It is the only Markdown file under Docs/, and the rules say there should be none.
