How the cards work

A separable verb is learned in contrast sets: two or three verbs that are particle opposites (aandoen/uitdoen) or that share a stem and are told apart by one visible thing (opruimen/schoonmaken). Every drawing in a set is the same picture with one thing changed.

A card has two halves. The text half shows the infinitive, the split form in one main clause (ik bel jou op) and the perfect (heeft opgebeld). The picture half is the scene brief. Each card gets one drawing in one panel: a change is drawn mid-action, with a small arrow where the direction is the difference. Alt text never names the verb, its finite form, its participle, its particle at the end of a clause, or any form of its partner.

The perfect is written out because the ge- sits between particle and stem (opgebeld, meegenomen) and the auxiliary is not always hebben (is ingestapt, is aangekomen). Forms were checked against tools/lexicon/werkwoorden-vormen.tsv (Bijlage 3b) for the stem verbs; the base verb of each compound is listed there when irregular.

How a set was placed

One rule places every set, whatever batch it came from. A set sits in the band of its most frequent member (A0 up to 250, A1 251–1000, A2 1001–2500, B1 2501–5000, B2 5001–10000). A set with no member ranked within 10000 sits in B2, the last band. No separable verb ranks inside the top 250 (the nearest, mee-eten, is 259), so A0 is empty; A1 is empty for the reason in its lead.

A promotion moves a set to an earlier band and needs a stated daily-life reason; 21 sets are promoted. Promotions are common here because the lexicon undercounts compound separables (opendoen 8519, instappen 7644, opbellen 8777): it files their split forms under the base verb. 1 homograph entry moves a set later, because the member that ranks it counts mostly another sense. Nothing else moves a set. select.py refuses an entry that does not move the set, and a promotion whose stated rank is wrong.

Ranks are lemma ranks, so a homograph lends its other senses to the set. Where the band would still hold without the borrowed sense it is left alone: wissel (invallen 4866, toekijken 4959), tent (opzetten 2407 also counts een bril opzetten and koffie opzetten; afbreken 4722 keeps the set no later than B1).

Everyday items sit at the lowest sensible level, A2 at the highest (owner rule). Where rank puts a set of daily-life verbs (home, food, clothes, transport) in B1 or B2, the set is promoted to A2 and its reason is stated as judgment, not lexicon data. Each entry below lists every member's niveau, top2000 and herkomst from tools/lexicon/woorden.tsv and its rank from woorden-frequentie.tsv; most compound separables have no row there at all. Verbs that belong to Werkwoorden

vertrekken and landen are not separable and already have cards in Werkwoorden (a2-regular, a1-regular), so this family does not reuse them. aankomen now pairs with wegrijden. opstijgen has no separable opposite (neerkomen reads as a crash) and is listed as a SKIP. ondergaan (the sun) was dropped with opkomen; the slug is also the Werkwoorden row for the inseparable ondergaan (to undergo, heeft ondergaan). The checker fails on any card id that is already a card in another family or a row in the Werkwoorden source files, and on any of our participles that another family uses as a headword.

Owner rulings of 20 November

  • Bijvoeglijke naamwoorden keeps opgepompt and leeggelopen, so oppompen and leeglopen are dropped here, and their partners opblazen and vollopen with them.
  • Werkwoorden keeps pinnen (a bank card at a terminal), so the ticket-gate pair is drawn by the way through the open flaps, not by a card on a reader.
  • Sunrise and sunset, and getting on and off a bike, cannot be told apart as stills by one changed element, so both sets are SKIPs.
  • The dog set (weglopen/terugkomen) stays: Bijwoorden drops hierheen/daarheen, which drew the same lawn.

Left out on purpose

  • Abstract separables with no honest single picture: voorkomen, voorstellen, uitmaken, plaatsvinden, aannemen as to assume, afvragen, omgaan, inzetten and similar.
  • A second sense of a verb: ophangen is the phone, aannemen is accepting a gift, opzoeken is looking up a word.
  • Pairs whose partner is not a real verb: opstaan/gaan zitten, opruimen/rommelen.

  • opstappen (B2, fiets): getting on and getting off a bike pass through the same poses, so the two stills differ only in the arrow, and any honest cue (which foot is down, the lean of the bike, the rider rising or sinking) changes more than one element.
