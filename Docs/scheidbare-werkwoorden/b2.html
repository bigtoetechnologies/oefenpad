a large spoon lifting pasta from the pan onto the plate.
Avoid
pasta and water poured into a colander (afgieten); a person eating up a plate of food (opeten); a plate set down at a laid table (klaarzetten); a stew stirred on a stove (not the card).
Contrast
Pair with afgieten. The only difference is where the pasta goes: onto the plate here, into the colander in the sink there.
Split form
schep ... op
Example
Ik schep voor iedereen pasta op.
I serve up pasta for everyone.
Perfect
heeft opgeschept
