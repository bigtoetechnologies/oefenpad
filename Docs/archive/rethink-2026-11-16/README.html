Rethink: structure and art

The 16 November structure proposal, superseded on structure.

Archived 16 November 2026. A design proposal, never wired into the site. Docs/product.html §11 (home = a tally of words met and held, plus one obvious next action) and §12 (themes, not goals, are the navigation) settle what this folder was arguing about, so the five-goal taxonomy below is superseded. Keep it for the art treatment and the three prototypes, not for the structure.

A working proposal, 16 November 2026. Nothing here is wired into the site. The three pages are standalone prototypes on the live tokens and the live scene art, so they can be compared like design-options/ was. The folder is scratch: promote or delete it once the direction is settled.

What prompted it

Two complaints, and they turn out to have one cause.

  1. The Voorzetselpaden art changed to style A because the brush could not be kept consistent across a batch. The home page is still option G, brush and heavy editorial type. Inside a game the two meet: a rounded, friendly pictogram on a page of hard rules and painted blocks.
  2. The home page shows six equal tiles. It has no way to say that the games train different things, that some have levels, or where the learner is. The thirteen-game plan in game-catalogue.html is goal-first; the interface is not.

The cause: the home page is a shop window for games, and the paint is doing the job a picture should do. There is no picture on the way in, so the first one a learner meets is inside a game, out of context and in another hand.

Evidence

shots/home-1400.png is the live page. shots/prep-discover-1400.png and shots/prep-choose-1400.png are the live game. The game's own three scenes are consistent with each other (shots/prep-choose-1400.png); the mismatch is between the drawing and the chrome.

Two things on the live home page are also not real:

What it says Where Reality
"4 dagen op rij" index.html, .telling Hardcoded. A first-time visitor is told they have a streak
"Je was gebleven bij op het station" index.html, Woordzoeker tile Hardcoded. Nothing reads progress

assets/site/oefenpad.js never touches either; the only storage it reads is the language choice. Accounts and game progress exist (GET /api/games/{game}/progress), so both can be real — but not from a static index.html.

The three prototypes

File What it tests Verdict
A a-sporen.html The games grouped by what they train, with the band ladder where one exists The answer to "different learning goals". Better as the index than as the home
B b-les.html The home as today's ten-minute path: three steps across three goals Recommended as the home page
C c-vandaag.html The home as one picture and one exercise; everything else below the fold Recommended for how the art is treated, not as the whole home

All three are checked at 1400 × 1000 and 430 × 932. Screenshots: shots/a-1400.png, shots/b-1400.png, shots/c-1400.png and the -430 versions.

A — Sporen

Four tracks, one per goal — Woorden, Spelling, Betekenis, Spreken — with the games as rows inside them. A game that has CEFR bands shows them (A0 4/10, A1 account); a game without bands says how you choose instead (Kies een categorie). This is the screen that makes the portfolio legible: it can hold thirteen games without changing shape, and it says what each one is for.

It is too long to be the first screen, and it still makes the learner choose.

B — Les

The home page is a session: three steps, one per goal, ten minutes, in an order. The current step is the only orange thing on the page. The other steps are outlined buttons, so orange keeps meaning "do this now". The picture appears on the step that needs it — the Voorzetselpaden step — at the size a phone choice tile uses (about 93 px), which is what style A was drawn for.

This is the structure that matches the name and the promise: oefenpad, ten minutes a day, not a shelf of games. It also gives the account something to save: which step you reached.

C — Vandaag

One plate, one sentence, one orange button. The drawing is 320 px wide on paper, not in a card, not on a painted block. The page reads as a picture book plate, and the heavy type sits beside it as caption. This is the direct answer to the art complaint: put the picture on the way in, give it air, and it stops looking like clipart inside an editorial page.

Below it, four goal cards are the only navigation. That part is weaker than A.

Recommendation

Combine them, in this order:

  1. Home = B. Today's path, three steps, real progress, one orange action. Steps are composed server-side from the goals the learner has not practised recently, so it does not need a scheduler to start.
  2. Index = A. Oefeningen becomes the goal map. The home's "Liever zelf kiezen?" links to it. This is where thirteen games can live.
  3. Art = C's treatment everywhere. A drawing is a plate on paper with air around it, never a decoration inside a tile or on a painted block.

One art register, not two

Retire the brush as the illustration style. Keep it as the brand and the interface's action/status paint. Reasons:

  • The brush cannot be produced consistently at scale. That is not a Grok problem, it is the nature of a hand-drawn line: 91 scenes now, 72 reels and hundreds of word images later. Style A holds a batch together.
  • A scene is a picture for a learner to read; the grid is the structure for them to navigate. Letting each do its own job is not a style clash, it is a division of labour.
  • The prototypes have almost no paint — only the logo and the orange action — and still read as Oefenpad. The grid and the type carry the identity. The large painted tiles were competing with the drawings.

Concretely, this changes game-art.html: the style A section becomes the rule for all game art, not only Voorzetselpaden. The brush section stays as the spec for the logo and the painted interface fills, which is a different job. designguide.html's "Illustration and game art" table ("the brush is the line") has to be rewritten.

Goal taxonomy

The learner-facing taxonomy is five goals, not the book's eight pillars:

Goal Games now Games planned
Woorden Geheugen, Koppelen Flitskaarten, Woordzoeker/Galgje with picture clues
Spelling Woordzoeker, Galgje —
Betekenis Voorzetselpaden Valse vrienden, Vul de leegte, Lego-woorden
Zinnen — Zinsbouwer, Vul de leegte
Spreken Verhaalmachine Luisteroefening (blocked on audio)

Pillars stay the content taxonomy (the book's chapters). The "eight pillars by motif" idea in decisions.html was never built and is not needed to distinguish five goals in a list. Motifs can come later as one small drawing per goal, in style A, if the labels test badly.

What it costs

Work Where
Home page reads progress index.html is static. Either make it a Razor Page or add a small client fetch of /api/games + /api/games/{game}/progress. Accounts already persist progress
Game metadata: goal, band, level count, availability client/src/site/GameList.tsx hardcodes six entries with kind. Move it to the API (/api/games) so the home, the index and GameShell share one source
Plate component The mask technique exists in client/src/styles/source/prepositions.css; lift .beeld / .plaat into oefenpad.css so the home can use it
Session composer A small server rule: pick three steps across goals, shortest unfinished first. It can start as a fixed rotation, no scheduling
Bilingual home The prototypes are Dutch-only to keep the comparison about structure. The live home still ships both languages

No new art is needed for any of this. The one picture in the prototypes is already in assets/voorzetselpaden/.

Open, for the owner

  1. Is the product "pick a game" or "do today's practice"? B says the second. If it is the first, A is the home page and this rethink is smaller.
  2. Does the home show progress to a guest? B needs a step to start from even with no history; the prototype shows a sample signed-in state.
  3. One art register? Retiring the brush for content art is a bigger call than changing the home page, and it contradicts game-art.html today.
  4. Which goal names are canonical? Woorden / Spelling / Betekenis / Spreken is what the live tiles already say, minus the missing labels. It is four words a beginner can meet on day one, which is the point.
  5. If "multiple language games" was meant as other learner languages rather than the several Dutch games, that contradicts decisions.html and is a separate decision.
