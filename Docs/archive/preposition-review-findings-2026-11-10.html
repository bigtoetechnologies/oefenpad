10 November 2026, audit; same day, every step of the recommendation applied. The audit ran against the rule in prepositions-art-handover-2026-11-10.html and the curriculum in prepositions-roadmap.html. Three further defects the first pass missed are marked New below and were fixed in the same sweep.

Scope: every tracked frame in the three art plans plus the 30 shipped pilot frames.

Bottom line

Every frame has an English explanation and a Scene/art note, and there are zero greeting/formula fakes — tot ziens was correctly removed. Every blocking fault found in the audit has been fixed:

  • The 36 B1 frames with no bare preposition were resolved: 29 bare separable verbs were replaced and the 7 pronominal-adverb frames stay.
  • The 17 B2 batch blocks that had drifted from their tracker rows were re-synced to the tracker.
  • The 9 prepositions in roadmap §7 that no frame used are now framed in B2-46.

ten einde te (#43) was the last off-rule frame and has since been replaced by ten dienste staan van. The plans are ready to send to Grok.

Correction to the handover: it says "B1 has 32 frames with no preposition — 13 in B1-07/08". The real number is 36, and 17 of them are in B1-07/08. The handover misses the pronominal-adverb group in B1-07. Of the 36, 29 are bare separable verbs and 7 are pronominal-adverb frames (see §3).

Two consequences:

  • The file mismatches (#115, 121, 124, 125, 130, 142, 143, 146, 147, 148, 176, 239) fork the asset name from the tracker, so the shipped scene id would not resolve.
  • functioneren als (#121) is not a preposition frame at all. als is not in this curriculum's preposition set, and it contradicts the tracker's leidinggeven aan.

Also wrong, both fixed: B2-13's CONTRAST PAIRS said "One, four, five, eight, nine, ten and 11 are singles"; the batch has ten subjects. New: B2-26 has the same fault and the first pass missed it — "Nine, ten and 11 are handing-over singles" went on to describe a third image, "a folder pushed across a desk", that no subject in the batch asks for. An artist working from that block would have drawn an eleventh picture.

Aside from the drift, the frames use standard Dutch collocations. The unusual ones were spot-checked and are valid: beschikken tot (#53), noodzaken tot, bewogen worden tot, benodigd zijn tot, zich verlagen tot. A full native collocation pass is beyond what a script can prove.

3. Are any frames not really prepositions? (the tot ziens question)

No greetings or formulae anywhere. A search of all 710 frames (including the pilot) for tot ziens, tot morgen, tot straks, tot later, tot nu toe, tot volgende week and hoe gaat het returned zero hits. prepositions-art-a0-a2.md L58–68 documents the removal and what replaced it. This part is already correct.

What was off-rule fell into three buckets. The first two have been fixed; the third stays.

(a) 29 bare separable verbs in B1 (replaced)

The list in §1 minus the 7 B1-07 pronominals: opbellen, afwassen, invullen, ophangen, zich vervelen, oprotten, afronden, inpakken, uitpakken, zich aankleden, vergeten, … None governs a preposition. All 29 were replaced on 10 November.

(b) 16 bare/other verbs in B2 batch blocks (fixed)

The §2 mismatches, minus #227 (which is a wording loss, not a bare verb). Notably functioneren als, een contract beëindigen, opvangen, een taxatie laten uitvoeren, loslaten, aandragen, afronden, inplannen, inwerken, vergoeden, ingrijpen. All 16 blocks were re-synced to their tracker chunk.

(c) 8 pronominal-adverb frames — keep these

ergens naartoe, waar … vandaan, erover nadenken, eraan toe zijn (B1-07) and ergens klaar mee zijn (#403). These have no bare preposition but carry one inside a pronominal adverb. Roadmap §6 budgets 40 frames for "Pronominal adverbs"; they are in scope. The fix is to add pronominal adverbs to the handover's frame rule (L23–28), not to replace the frames.

Conclusion: the tot ziens class is gone, and the 29 B1 separables, 16 B2 block-side bare verbs and als in #121 have all been fixed. The last non-preposition frame, ten einde te (#43), was replaced by ten dienste staan van.

4. Are any verb+preposition chunks missing?

This is judgement-based: the repo has no frequency list (roadmap §12.1 says re-cut everything when one arrives). Cross-checked against all 568 chunks and the 30 pilots at audit time, 23 common chunks appeared nowhere:

wonen in, gaan naar, kiezen voor, hopen op, wijzen op, vertrouwen op, bestaan uit, leiden tot, akkoord gaan met, het eens zijn met, gebaseerd zijn op, afkomstig zijn uit, vergelijken met, het verschil tussen, passen op, afgaan op, zich verlaten op, ontstaan uit, ruimte voor, tellen als, werken voor, twijfelen tussen, zich zorgen maken over.

  • kiezen voor was the standout: the corpus framed kiezen tussen / kiezen uit, opteren voor and beslissen tot, but never the most common form. It is now B1 #73.
  • werken voor is deliberately excluded (too close to werken bij). The positive zich zorgen maken over was absent while zich geen zorgen maken over (#401) was present; it is now B1 #76.
  • Beware substring false positives: gaan naar is not covered by uitgaan naar (#82), nor wijzen op by afwijzen op grond van (#354), nor leiden tot by opleiden tot (#149), nor passen op by toepassen op (#336).

Fifteen of the 23 are now framed, mostly by the B1 replacement: wonen in, gaan naar, kiezen voor, hopen op, wijzen op, vertrouwen op, bestaan uit, akkoord gaan met, het eens zijn met, gebaseerd zijn op, vergelijken met, passen op, ruimte voor, twijfelen tussen and zich zorgen maken over. Eight remain absent: leiden tot, afkomstig zijn uit, ontstaan uit, het verschil tussen, afgaan op, zich verlaten op, tellen als and werken voor. The last two are deliberate exclusions; the other six are the frequency-list backlog. Three further common chunks are also absent — antwoorden op, wijzen naar and gewend zijn aan — though gewend aan is already framed (#25, #26,

39).

Preposition coverage gaps (roadmap §7)

9 prepositions were never framed anywhere at audit time: zonder, vanwege, gezien, richting, per, aangaande, betreffende, jegens, overeenkomstig.

  • zonder was absent entirely (handover L49 and roadmap §7 both flag it).
  • vanwege, gezien, richting, per are roadmap §7 phase D (batches 20–25); aangaande, betreffende, jegens, overeenkomstig are phase E (26–31). Both phases are B2, and no B2 frame used any of them. That was a roadmap gap, not just an art-plan gap.
  • met inbegrip van and ten opzichte van are present, so §7's E-row was only partly unmet.

All nine are now framed in B2-46 (#451–#460), and the roadmap's batch 32 records the close-out.

tot appears 18 times, but all in B2 — first at B2 #13 zich richten tot. The handover's claim that tot has no frame below B2 is correct; the summary's "first at #23" is off by ten rows.

B1 first-introduces many prepositions roadmap §7 assigns to A2 batch 5 (achter, bij, binnen, boven, buiten, langs, na, naar, naast, om, onder, over, rond, tussen, van, vanaf). That is consistent with handover decision 2 ("keep the B1-01/02 spatial frames — they're the only frames teaching those words"), but it means A0–A2 covers only aan, door, in, met, op, tegen, uit, voor.

Duplicates that are likely by design

Duplicate chunk strings are mostly intentional contrast variants. Notable repeats: B1 denken aan ×11, zorgen voor ×4; B2 zich beroepen op ×4, zich richten tot ×3. A few look like pure duplicates rather than contrast pairs (bereid tot #183/#184, geschikt voor #185/#186, rekening houden met

224/#225). That is a curation call, not an error.

Filenames

No duplicate filenames in any file; all ASCII. Pilot filename references resolve against assets/voorzetselpaden/ and prepositions.json.

Reproducing the checks

cd repos/oefenpad

# Row and fence counts (a0-a2 20/4, b1 200/40, b2 460/92)
for f in Docs/prepositions-art-a0-a2.md Docs/prepositions-art-b1.md Docs/prepositions-art-b2.md; do
  echo "$f $(grep -c '^````' "$f") $(awk -F'|' '/^\| [0-9]+ \|/ && $2 ~ /^ *[0-9]+ *$/ {c++} END{print c+0}' "$f")"
done

# Duplicate filenames (expect empty)
awk -F'|' '/^\| [0-9]+ \|/ && $2 ~ /^ *[0-9]+ *$/ {f=$3; gsub(/^ +| +$/,"",f); print f}' \
  Docs/prepositions-art-b2.md | sort | uniq -d

The no-preposition, tracker↔block, duplicate-Scene and coverage checks were run with a small Python parser over the tracker tables and the ## Batch subject blocks. Key points for re-running:

  • Whitelist tegenover — it is a real preposition, so a naive scan falsely flags #358. Whitelist the pronominal forms too (naartoe, vandaan, mee, toe, and any er…/daar…/waar… chunk), or the scan reports the eight pronominal frames as failures. Before the 10 November replacements it also reported 36 B1 failures; after them it is clean.
  • Compare the tracker Scene cell against the block Scene as well as the filename and chunk. It is a condensed copy, so match it as a prefix and read the misses by hand: most are legitimate condensations and ten were stale.
  • Block subjects renumber from 1, so compare block subject N to tracker row batch_start + N − 1.
  • Only treat a backticked token as a filename if it is ASCII-hyphenated or ends in .png; backticks in the Pair/note column are usually prepositions.
