9 November 2026, revised 10 November. Read prepositions-roadmap.html for the curriculum and game-art.html for the drawing rules. preposition-review-findings-2026-11-10.html is the audit this revision came from.

What this is

The prepositions game shipped with 30 frames. The roadmap takes it to 710 (A0–A2 50, B1 200, B2 460). Three art plans now hold the missing frames as copy-paste Grok batches of ten. All 680 planned frames govern a preposition (the fused-preposition frames ten slotte and ten einde te were replaced on 10 November), so every batch is ready to send.

File State
Docs/prepositions-art-a0-a2.md 20 new frames, done
Docs/prepositions-art-b1.md 200 frames, done; 29 replaced 10 Nov
Docs/prepositions-art-b2.md 460 frames, re-synced and extended 10 Nov
Docs/prepositions-roadmap.html The 710-frame ladder

Nothing had been drawn or sent to Grok when this handover was written. The game JSON (server/Oefenpad.Resources/Games/Data/prepositions.json) held 30 scenes and 18 levels, nothing was committed, and main was 9 commits ahead.

Update, 10 November (evening). The A0–A2 batch (prepositions-art-a0-a2.md, 20 frames) was drawn and shipped: the masks are in assets/voorzetselpaden/, the JSON now has 50 scenes, 50 frames and 33 levels, and a review sheet is built by tools/build-prepositions-sheet.py. The B1 and B2 plans are still undrawn.

The rule for a frame

It must be a chunk that governs a preposition — verb (letten op), adjective (bang voor), noun (zin in) or a fixed idiom. Not a bare verb, not a free adjunct (zonder afspraak), not a greeting (tot ziens). Both were removed for this reason.

A pronominal adverb counts. erover nadenken, eraan toe zijn, ergens naartoe, waar … vandaan and ergens klaar mee zijn carry their preposition fused into er…, daar… or waar… rather than standing before a noun. Roadmap §6 budgets 40 frames for them, so they are in scope and a "has a preposition?" scan must whitelist them. Eight such frames exist today: seven in B1-07 and ergens klaar mee zijn (B2 #403).

What was done on 10 November

The audit in preposition-review-findings-2026-11-10.html found three classes of fault and the owner settled the three policy questions the same day.

The 29 B1 separables are gone. B1-08 was rebuilt whole as the high-frequency everyday chunks the set was missing (gaan naar, wonen in, kiezen voor, twijfelen tussen, hopen op, zich zorgen maken over, betalen voor, uitgeven aan, vertrouwen op, bellen met), and nineteen more were replaced in B1-11…B1-20. The prefix material moved to the Uit Elkaar game idea in suggestions.html, which now carries the word list. Roadmap batch 8 and the B1 "What B1 adds" table were changed to match.

Replacing a frame is four edits, not one: the tracker row, the batch subject block, the CONTRAST PAIRS line, and every other row whose Pair/note names it. Ten neighbouring notes were repointed this way (#105, #119, #134, #159, #174,

185 in B1, plus four CP prose lines in B2). B1-08's prompt preamble also still

told the artist to match the separable prefix; its TASK and VALIDATION text is now the standard preposition wording.

B2-46 closes the coverage gap. zonder, vanwege, gezien, richting, per, aangaande, betreffende, jegens and overeenkomstig had no frame anywhere; ten new frames teach them the way B2-44 teaches sinds and tijdens. B2 is 460 frames in 46 batches, and the roadmap gained a batch 32 for it.

ten einde te (#43) was the last fused-preposition frame after ten slotte; it was replaced by ten dienste staan van, which keeps B2-05's ten … van theme and governs its van.

tot still has no frame below B2 — its first is B2 #13 zich richten tot, and all 18 of its frames are B2. via appears only inside verspreiden via. Neither is a blocker; both are recorded in roadmap §7.

Two decisions the owner has given, kept here because they are easy to re-open:

  1. Keep the B1-01/02 spatial frames and B2-44 (behalve, sinds, tijdens, volgens, wegens, ondanks) despite roadmap §11 excluding free use. They are the only frames teaching those words. B2-46 follows the same logic.
  2. Keep B2's ~68 separable-prefix chunks (aandacht vragen voor). They govern real prepositions, so they are legal frames; only bare separables were cut.

Checking an edit

F=Docs/prepositions-art-b2.md
grep -c '^````' $F            # must be 2 × number of batches
awk -F'|' '/^\| [0-9]+ \|/ && $2 ~ /^ *[0-9]+ *$/ {c++} END{print c}' $F
awk -F'|' '/^\| [0-9]+ \|/ && $2 ~ /^ *[0-9]+ *$/ {f=$3; gsub(/^ +| +$/,"",f); print f}' $F | sort | uniq -d

B2 currently: 460 rows, 46 batches, 92 fences, no duplicate filenames and no tracker↔block mismatches. B1: 200 rows, 20 batches, 40 fences, no duplicate Scene cells. Across all three files there are no duplicate filenames and no no-preposition rows once the pronominal forms and tegenover are whitelisted. preposition-review-findings-2026-11-10.html has the parser notes for re-running the tracker↔block and no-preposition checks.

Traps

  • Files are long; find ## Batch line numbers with grep -n and read with sed -n 'A,Bp'. Edit only with exact matches.
  • Batch prompts use four-backtick fences. An unbalanced fence breaks the copy-paste block without warning.
  • Keep the tracker row and the batch subject block identical — B2 was broken because only one side got updated.
  • Filenames are ASCII; chunk text keeps its diacritics.
  • A new scene is easy to write against the house style. Two drafts this round had to be redone: one distinguished two cloths by stripe pattern, which game-art.html forbids (no hair, clothing pattern or fingers), and one named a subject as a pair member in one line and a single in the next.
  • A scene must not duplicate an existing one. Check the Scene column for the same prop before writing — a wall clock was proposed for #454 when binnen-de-gestelde-termijn already owns it.
  • Never claim a check passed without running it.
