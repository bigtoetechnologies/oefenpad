ZiN and Heard Dutch Here's verb–preposition lists, checked against ours

The 15 November comparison of both lists against ours.

Checked 15 November 2026 against

  • https://zininnederlands.be/leer-nederlands/woordleer-nederlands/het-verbum/werkwoorden-met-vast-voorzetsel/ ("Werkwoorden met vast voorzetsel", 90 entries), and
  • https://www.heardutchhere.net/verbs_prepositions.html (Marco Schuffelen, "Verbs and Their Prepositions", 187 entries).

The question asked was whether the plan holds everything these two pages list, whether either page shows a mistake of ours, and — where something is missing — which band it belongs in.

What the two sources are, and what they are not

Neither page gives levels. ZiN is a Belgian teaching site, so a few entries are Belgian usage (bang zijn van beside bang zijn voor). Heard Dutch Here is one man's collection and says so: "It is of course not possible to put all verbs and all possible verb-preposition combinations on this page." So absence from either list proves nothing on its own, and presence is not a level.

Both are useful for the one thing they do well: naming combinations we might have missed, and catching a chunk that teaches the wrong preposition.

The audit brought 129 entries into the plan — 33 from ZiN, 96 from Heard Dutch Here — which is 121 distinct chunks, eight entries naming the same chunk on both lists. Every one of those 121 is a proposal card: no art, no data-verdict, data-status="planned".

Measurement is tools/_vp_tmp/coverage.py: NFKD-normalise both sides, drop zich/zijn/hebben/iemand/iets/het/een/de/er, then compare the first and last surviving tokens. That sees zich aanpassen aan = aanpassen aan and still keeps denken aan apart from denken om.

1. Faults the two lists exposed, and what was done

1.1 ruiken aan was missing, and with it the everyday sense

a2.html carried ruiken naar ("to smell of") alone. Heard Dutch Here has both, and they are not interchangeable: ruiken naar is what a thing smells like (Het ruikt naar regen), ruiken aan is what a person does with their nose (Hij rook aan de fles). The page taught the pair's wrong half. ruiken aan is now on a2.html beside it; the section heads with 2 prepositions, 2 cards.

1.2 Section counts said "1 prepositions, 1 cards"

The count line above each contrast set was built from the number of chunk groups, not from the prepositions in the heading and the cards in the section. So a cluster of three chunks that all take aan (aandacht besteden aan, tijd besteden aan, besteden aan) read "3 prepositions", and a one-card section read "1 cards". Both now read the truth: 1 preposition, 3 cards. Fixed in the loader and re-stamped on every section this audit touched.

A later check of all fifty-seven count lines — comparing each one against its own <span class="preps"> and its own cards — found five still wrong in sections the audit had not touched: uitkijken (7 cards, is 8), vertrouwen (2, is 3), berusten (2, is 3), inwerken ("2 prepositions", there is one), and verdedigen ("3 prepositions", there are two). All five are corrected. The stamp now holds for every contrast set on all eight pages.

1.3 Six cards put meaning in writing

A self-review after the bulk insert found six scene briefs that only worked if the learner could read something inside the picture or on a printed page: bedoelen-met-dat-woord (a word on a page), recht-hebben-op-een-vergoeding (a letter), zich-verbazen-over-de-prijs (a price tag), strijden-tegen-de-ziekte (a banner), zeker-zijn-van-de-uitslag and dreigen-met-ontslag ("printed …"). All six now carry blank surfaces and name what the drawing shows instead. The site rule is no text inside artwork, ever, because one art set has to serve every first language.

1.4 The same chunk twice

geinteresseerd in and geïnteresseerd zijn in were both on a2.html. They were merged into geïnteresseerd zijn in (four examples). interesse hebben in is the noun-first twin and is now a separate card on a2-plus.html, since it is a different sentence shape, not a duplicate.

1.5 Reported but not a fault

The project rules, item 12, said the praten-tegen card on a2.html still answered "Ja" to a question whose scene says no. Checked: praten-tegen-muur answers Nee, and so do the other three praten tegen cards. The stale sentence is gone from the project rules. Nothing in the pages needed changing.

1.6 Two entries checked and kept

  • kijken op (b1.html) is kept against the drop rule, and the reason is written into the card's note: op is a complement here (op de klok kijken, op de kaart kijken), not a free adjunct like op in afspreken op een tijd.
  • getuigen over (b1-plus.html) is kept beside getuigen van, which is a real contrast: getuigen over is a witness speaking about something, getuigen van is a thing being evidence of something.
  • getuigen over tested against the normaliser — no change needed.

3. Deliberately not added

3.1 Near-equivalences of a chunk already in the plan

Entry Why not
gebruikmaken van (ZiN) Same chunk as gebruik maken van (B1+), written as one word. Two cards would teach a spelling, not a preposition.
danken voor Our plan teaches bedanken voor (A1). danken alone is the formal/literary verb.
zin hebben in (ZiN) In the plan as zin in (A0 and A2+).
bang zijn van (ZiN) Belgian spoken variant of bang voor (A0). In Netherlands Dutch bang van niet is a different idiom entirely, and the site teaches Netherlands Dutch.
geïnteresseerd zijn in Already at A2 with four examples; interesse hebben in was added beside it rather than replacing it.

3.2 The 12 HDH entries still absent, with reasons

These are the only entries of the 187 that have no chunk. Each one was read in the source with its example sentence before being left out.

Entry Their gloss and example Why it stays out
aankunnen op to count on, rely on — Je kunt erop aan Same meaning as rekenen op (A2 pairs, B1) and vertrouwen op (A2+), and aan is a separable prefix that splits to the end in a main clause, so a card could not build a statement with the chunk as listed.
aanraken met touching with — aanraken met een stok met is the instrument, free with any tool. Not a fixed combination.
blijven in to stay, remain in — Ik ben in Amerika gebleven Free locative. blijven bij is the fixed one and is in the plan.
eten met eating with — knife and fork, or friends met is instrument or company, free.
eten van eating from — eten van een bord Container choice is not fixed: van het bord and uit de pan are both ordinary. No rule to teach. drinken uit (A0) stays because drinking is nearly always out of a bottle or glass.
gaan met going by (transport) — Ga je met de auto? met is the means of transport, free. The fixed frame here is hoe gaat het met …, which is a different teaching point.
komen voor to come for — We komen voor de muziek voor is the purpose, free. The "to occur" sense their list does not mean is the separable voorkomen, a different verb.
schieten naar shooting at a single target — schoot een pijl naar de boom naar is direction, op is aim. schieten op (B2, added) carries the fixed sense; two cards would blur it.
toegeven van admitting to — het moeten toegeven van fouten Not a verb + preposition at all: it is a nominalisation (het toegeven van fouten), the same van any infinitive takes.
vallen van to fall from — van de trap gevallen Free ablative. vallen op and vallen uit are the fixed ones and are in the plan.
willen naar to want to go to — Ik wil naar huis Free destination.
willen van to want of — Wat wil je toch van mij? Real, but it lives in one question frame, and a card builds statements. Not teachable in this format.

4. What this does not settle

  • The 121 new cards have no art. They are proposals with not drawn yet, so tools/import-prepositions-band.py cannot see them and the game does not ship them. 271 of the 1015 cards are in that state.
  • The matrix pages count fewer chunks than the band pages. That is by design, not a bug: tools/build-prepositions-matrix.py leaves out cards marked data-verdict="drop" (b1 129 of 141, B1+ 199 of 256, B2 138 of 212).
  • 136 older cards have no English gloss. Every card on the pages has a <p class="gloss">, but on a0 (28), a1 (60) and a2 (48) it is empty. Those cards predate this audit and none of the 121 added here is among them; their only English is in the scene brief and the example translation. Left for a separate pass.
  • The game data was not regenerated. prepositions.json is unchanged by this audit; regenerating it from the pages would ship unfinished proposals and would re-introduce the old praten-tegen wording.
  • The A1 gate is unchanged. wachten tot, geven aan and zwaaien naar are behind the same account wall as the rest of A1.
  • Levels are my judgement. Neither source has levels. A2/A2+/B1 placements follow the existing split: concrete-and-frequent at A2, adjective and noun frames at A2+, the abstract and the nominal at B1 and up. delen door sits at B1+ rather than A2 because it belongs in the delen contrast set, and contrast beats level.
