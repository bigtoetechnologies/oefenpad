How the cards are grouped

By contrast set. wachten op sits beside wachten voor, because the pair is what teaches: two drawings differing on one visible point. Under each preposition its own examples sit together.

If the partner sits in another band, the heading links to it. Chunks with no partner appear under Single frames.

Every card carries a reference number. The numbers run on across the eight files, from #1 in A0 through the last card in B2, rather than restarting in each band, so a reviewer can name a single frame wherever it sits.

A0, A1 and A2

The fifty A0–A2 frames sat in one band. They are split: A0 is survival in the first weeks, A1 the concrete sets with their first contrast pairs, A2 the abstract and emotional work plus the separable and pronominal chunks.

A0 deliberately has no contrast set: two prepositions on one verb require the learner to hold both already.
