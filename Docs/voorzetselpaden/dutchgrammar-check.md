# dutchgrammar.com's verb–preposition list, checked against ours

Checked 13 November 2026 against
<https://www.dutchgrammar.com/en/?n=Verbs.Pr01> ("Verbs and prepositions").

## What was read, and how

The A–Z bar at the top of that page is **not a set of pages**. Every letter is
an anchor into the one page (`<a href="#A">`, `<a href="#B">`, …), and eight
letters (C, F, J, M, O, Q, X, Y) have no entries at all. So "all the pages
linked from the alphabet" is one page: 74 rows, 76 combinations because two
rows give two prepositions (`beginnen met, beginnen aan` and
`sturen aan, sturen naar`).

Their own note: *"The list of verb-preposition combinations is far from
complete."* That matters for every "we have something they don't" below.

The live site answers Cloudflare's managed challenge to non-browser clients
(HTTP 403), including through a text proxy. The most recent Wayback capture
that returns 200 is **30 December 2022**, and the page footer says *"Last
updated on October 13, 2022"* — so the snapshot is very likely still the
current text, but this was not verified against the live page.

## Our side

Eight band pages under `Docs/voorzetselpaden/`: 661 chunk groups, 894
`<article class="kaart">` cards, of which 160 carry `class="beeld"`. The game
ships the A0 and A1 cards that have art: 28 levels, 91 scenes. A0/A1 holds 30
chunks, 28 of which qualify as levels (`vragen om` and `doorgeven aan` have no
art).

## Result

Of their 76 combinations, **50 appear somewhere in our plan and 26 appear
nowhere**. Of our 30 A0/A1 chunks, **11 are on their list and 19 are not**.

No A0/A1 combination contradicts their list. Nothing we ship uses the wrong
preposition for a meaning they list.

## 3. Where their gloss and ours disagree

Only four are worth an edit, and three of those are on A1 cards — i.e. already
in the shipped game.

| Their entry | Their gloss | Ours | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **vragen om** | to ask for (a favour) | to ask for (A1) | **Worth changing.** The parenthetical is the whole point of the A1 contrast set: `vragen aan` = ask a person, `vragen naar` = ask about, `vragen om` = ask for a thing/favour. Without it the three look interchangeable in English. |
| **vragen naar** | to enquire about, to ask for (someone) | to ask about (A1) | **Worth changing.** Ours drops the "ask for a person" sense, which is the other half of the contrast with `vragen om`. |
| **praten tegen** | to talk to (someone) | to talk at (A2) | **Worth deciding.** "Talk at" is the hostile sense; theirs is neutral. The A2 card's Klopt answer is still "Ja" (open item 12 in `CLAUDE.md`), so this card needs attention anyway. |
| zoeken naar | to look for | to search for (A0) | **Worth changing** for a beginner gloss; "search for" is the literal translation, "look for" is the meaning. |
| praten met | to talk with (someone) | to talk with | Agreement |
| zeggen tegen | to say to | to say to | Agreement |
| uitkijken voor | to watch out for | to watch out for | Agreement |
| zorgen voor | to take care of | to care for (A0) | Compatible; theirs is the more common teaching gloss |
| denken aan | to think about | to think of (A2) | Compatible; our B1 gloss already says "to think about" |
| wijzen op | to point at | to point out (B1) | Both senses exist; not a fault |
| afgeven op | to come off on (paint), to complain about | to badmouth | Compatible; ours is the living idiom |

## 4. Where their list is unreliable

Do not copy their English. These are wrong or odd as written:

- `vragen aan | to ask to (someone)` — broken English; it means "to ask someone".
- `verlangen naar | to long to` — should be "to long for".
- `bezwaar hebben tegen | to object (something)` — should be "to object to something".
- `twijfelen over | to doubt about` — should be "to doubt" / "to be in doubt about".
- `afgeven op | to come off on (paint), to complain about` — two unrelated senses
  shoved into one gloss; "to speak ill of" is the everyday one.
- `sturen aan` — not a fixed combination in the way the rest of the list is;
  `sturen naar` is the one to teach.
- `zich voegen naar` — correct Dutch, but the English is not.

Their page is also explicitly incomplete, and visibly so at the bottom end:
it has no `lopen`, `fietsen`, `wonen`, `kopen`, `feliciteren`, `bedanken`,
`uitnodigen`, `meedoen`, or any of the separable motion verbs we teach at A0.

## 6. Faults this check flushed out that are not about their list

Found while comparing; listed here because they are cheap to action and none
of them is in the shipped A0/A1 slice.

**Chunk labels that are not verb–preposition combinations.** These sit in
A2+ and above and would need a redraw or a relabel before they can be taught
as a combination:

| Chunk | Card sentence | What is really going on |
|---|---|---|
| durven om | Hij durft eindelijk om hulp te vragen. | `om` belongs to `om hulp vragen`; `durven` takes a bare infinitive |
| afspreken op | Zij spreken af op een vaste tijd. | `op een vaste tijd` is an adverbial, not a fixed combination |
| leren met | Hij leert Nederlands met hele zinnen. | `met hele zinnen` is an instrument |
| controleren voor | Zij controleert de jaarrekening voor het bestuur. | `voor het bestuur` = on behalf of |
| behandelen bij | Het beroep wordt behandeld bij de rechter. | `bij de rechter` is a location |
| ophouden aan | Hij houdt de bezoeker op aan de uitgang. | separable `ophouden` + a place |
| uitvoeren volgens | — | `volgens` is a free preposition, not a complement |
| zorgen over | Hij heeft zorgen over het examen. | the chunk is `zorgen hebben over`, a noun plus preposition |

**Spelling duplicate.** `geinteresseerd in` (A2, no diaeresis) and
`geïnteresseerd zijn in` (A2) are the same combination written two ways. One
of them should go.

**Art gap.** `vragen om` and `doorgeven aan` are fully written A1 chunks with
zero images, so the importer correctly refuses to make them levels. `vragen om`
is one of the three-way contrast set mentioned in section 3; it is the most
conspicuous hole in the shipped A1 band.

**Count discrepancy.** The eight band pages hold 894 `<article class="kaart">`
cards (661 chunk groups), while `CLAUDE.md` says "802 active cards". The 802
figure looks stale; worth reconciling before it is quoted again.

## 7. What I would do with this

1. **Fix the A1 glosses** for `vragen om` and `vragen naar` on `a1.html`
   (section 3). No art needed. The band pages are master, so this is an owner
   edit — the importer would pick it up on the next run.
2. **Decide `praten tegen`** — "to talk at" or "to talk to" — together with the
   stale "Ja" on that card.
3. **Queue `geven aan`, `geven om`, `sturen naar`, `schreeuwen tegen`,
   `aftrekken van`, `zich aansluiten bij`, `zich abonneren op`,
   `bevestigen aan`** for A1/A2 once art exists, three scenes each.
4. **Keep their list out of the pipeline.** It is incomplete, it has no levels,
   and some of its English is wrong. It is useful as a cross-check, not as
   input: `Docs/voorzetselpaden/*.html` stays the master.
