Scene
a builder kneels on bare ground inside a rectangle marked out with four pegs and a taut string. One hand presses the very first brick into place at a corner, the other holds a trowel loaded with mortar, eyes down on the brick. 'Beginnen met' is equally correct Dutch for this scene, so the card cannot be scored against beginnen-met-de-les as a contrast. Teach it as a single.
