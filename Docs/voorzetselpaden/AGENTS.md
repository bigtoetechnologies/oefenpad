# Voorzetselpaden — working notes for agents

## The band pages are the source of truth

The eight band pages hold every chunk and every card:

`a0.html`, `a1.html`, `a2.html`, `a2-pairs.html`, `a2-plus.html`, `b1.html`,
`b1-plus.html`, `b2.html`

Edit those. They are the master copy of the content.

**`server/Oefenpad.Resources/Games/Data/prepositions.json` is generated output
for the game, not a source.** It is irrelevant to content decisions here. Never
edit it by hand, and do not regenerate it unless the owner asks. The importer
reads only A0/A1 cards that carry `class="beeld"` plus text; a run today would
reproduce the live ids and, as the one content delta, push the two A0/A1 gloss
fixes into the game.

`index.html` is hand-kept. `matrix-*.html` is generated by
`tools/build-prepositions-matrix.py` from the band pages.

## Conventions

- **A dropped card stays on the page.** Never delete the markup. Mark it
  `data-verdict="drop"` and put `<span class="v-drop">drop</span>` in
  `.vlaggen`, with a `<dt>Verdict</dt><dd class="verdict_why">` giving the
  reason. Commit `317f9db` did this to the 74 nonsense frames.
- **Dropped cards are excluded from `matrix-*.html`.** They also never reach
  the game in practice: no dropped card carries `class="beeld"`, and the
  importer skips cards without art. They are **included** in the band-page
  section teller (`p.contrastnoot`, e.g. "2 prepositions, 2 cards") and in the
  `index.html` totals; those count every card written on the page.
- `data-chunk` and `data-examples` live on `div.chunkgroep`, **not** on
  `<article class="kaart">`. The same label appears again in the card's own
  `<h3 class="chunk">`; update both when relabelling a chunk.
- A chunk group's gloss is the **first non-empty gloss** in the group, which is
  what the game takes. Later cards often repeat it and a few give each card its
  own variant, so when changing a chunk's gloss check the rest of the group.
- A card's `data-id` and the filename in its footer `<p class="id">` must agree
  with the scene and the example. Rename both together.
- `Contrast sets` groups chunks by shared stem; every cluster there has at
  least two chunk groups. A chunk with no contrast partner belongs under
  `Single frames`, where one cluster may hold one chunk group.

## Tools

```sh
python3 tools/prepositions-pages.py check                 # card / scene sanity
python3 tools/prepositions-pages.py read --out records.json
python3 tools/prepositions-pages.py patch fixes.json [--dry-run]
python3 tools/build-prepositions-matrix.py                # rebuild matrix-*.html
```

`patch` takes `{"fixes": [{"id": ..., "scene": ..., ...}]}` and edits fields in
place: `scene`, `must`, `avoid`, `note`, `tests`, `voorbeeld-nl`, `bouw`,
`controlevraag`, `contrast`, `pronominaal`, `alt`, `verdict_why`, plus `chunk`,
`gloss` and `verdict`. It does **not** rename ids or move cards between
sections; do those by hand.

## Parsing pitfalls

- The `grep` tool here silently fails on `\|` alternation. Use Python `re`.
- `class="kaart"` alone misses the 22 cards that carry a second class
  (`sev-weak-picture` or `sev-minor`). Match `kaart` anywhere in the class
  attribute.
- Do not split band pages on `</section>`; the counts differ. Split on
  `<section class="cluster"` and `<div class="chunkgroep"`.
- Short numbers also match the embedded base64 images. Mask
  `src="data:image/png;base64,…"` before searching or diffing.
