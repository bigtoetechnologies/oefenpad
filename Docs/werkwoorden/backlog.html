Word classes still to come

The werkwoorden pages are the second card family after Voorzetselpaden. The same shape, one card per item with a scene brief, is still owed for:

  • Bijvoeglijke naamwoorden — adjectives with a comparability the picture can show: groot, duur, leeg, nat, moe.
  • Bijwoorden — adverbs and adverbial phrases of time and manner: gisteren, straks, plotseling, meteen.
  • Zelfstandige naamwoorden — nouns with their article and plural, which is where the lexicon already carries verified data.
  • Vaste uitdrukkingen — fixed chunks that are not verb + preposition: op tijd, ter plekke, met opzet.
  • Scheidbare werkwoorden — the group above, but with their own brief: two halves, one drawing or two.
  • Vervoegingen — conjugation drills. On 20 November the owner decided that unpredictable forms are vocabulary: irregular cards now carry the past singular and the perfect. Regular forms, other persons and the other tenses stay out; whether a game drills them is still open (see Docs/verb-games-plan.html).
  • Voorbeeldzinnen — dropped from this pass on purpose. If they come back, they come back as a separate field, not as part of the picture brief.

To make this list smaller

Add a verb to tools/_ww_tmp/select.py (or lift it out of the deferred set), rerun it, write the scene row in tools/_ww_tmp/scenes/<page>.tsv, and rerun tools/build-verb-cards.py. The frame numbers renumber themselves.
