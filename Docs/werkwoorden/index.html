What these pages are

One card per Dutch verb, in its base form. Each card carries a picture brief: Scene (what is drawn), Must read (the one mark that has to be legible at thumbnail size), Avoid (the near-miss actions, each with the competing verb) and Alt text (Dutch, for a screen reader). The art is the Voorzetselpaden style: flat cobalt #2B50C8 on white, no text. The brief is in base-prompt.txt.

90 of 201 cards are drawn so far: a card counts as drawn when assets/verbs/<slug>.png exists, and the rest are proposals.

Irregular cards also carry the forms a learner cannot predict: the past singular and the perfect (auxiliary plus participle), taken from Bijlage 3b into tools/lexicon/werkwoorden-vormen.tsv. Regular cards carry the base form only, because -te/-de and ge- are one rule taught once. There are no example sentences and no contrast pairs in this pass.

How the bands were cut

By lexicon frequency rank, from tools/lexicon/woorden-frequentie.tsv: A0 up to 250, A1 up to 1000, A2 up to 2500, B1 up to 5000. Irregular and regular verbs are separate pages, because a learner meets them in a different order. The ranks were cross-checked against the coursebook appendices in /media/tony/Data/Dutch (Bijlage 3b for irregular verbs, Bijlage 4 for separable verbs), which is also what the cross-check in Docs/voorzetselpaden/ used.

What is not here

Separable verbs, reflexive verbs, a handful of sensitive or rare lemmas, and the regular verbs below the A2 and B1 cut: that is 445 verbs, listed with their reason on backlog.html. A further 153 selected verbs have no honest single drawing and are listed there too, under "Verbs the writer could not draw". The same page also tracks the other word classes still to be given cards.

Where the words came from

tools/_ww_tmp/select.py writes the selection; tools/build-verb-cards.py turns it, plus the scene notes in tools/_ww_tmp/scenes/, into these pages. Conventions and the rerun recipe are in AGENTS.html.
