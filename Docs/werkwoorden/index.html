What these pages are

One card per sense, in the verb's base form. Each card carries a picture brief: Scene (what is drawn), Must read (the one mark that has to be legible at thumbnail size), Avoid (the near-miss actions, each with the competing verb) and Alt text (Dutch, for a screen reader). The art is the Voorzetselpaden style: flat cobalt #2B50C8 on white, no text. The brief is in base-prompt.txt.

A verb with a second sense that needs its own drawing gets a second card, printed with the sense in brackets and filed under its own id: dragen (to carry) and dragen (kleding) (to wear). The pair shows two different actions, so a game must not serve both cards of a pair in one round.

333 of 347 cards are drawn so far: a card counts as drawn when assets/verbs/<slug>.png exists, and the rest are proposals.

Irregular cards also carry the forms a learner cannot predict: the past singular and the perfect (auxiliary plus participle), taken from Bijlage 3b into tools/lexicon/werkwoorden-vormen.tsv. Regular cards carry the base form only, because -te/-de and ge- are one rule taught once. There are no example sentences on these cards.

A contrast note appears only where a second verb shares the drawing and differs in one visible point. Where no such partner exists the card has no note; that is the normal case. A partner may sit in another band, because a verb is banded on its own frequency, not on the company it keeps.

How the bands were cut

By lexicon frequency rank, from tools/lexicon/woorden-frequentie.tsv: A0 up to 250, A1 up to 1000, A2 up to 2500, B1 up to 5000, B2 up to 10,000. Irregular and regular verbs are separate pages, because a learner meets them in a different order. The ranks were cross-checked against the coursebook appendices in /media/tony/Data/Dutch (Bijlage 3b for irregular verbs, Bijlage 4 for separable verbs), which is also what the cross-check in Docs/voorzetselpaden/ used.

Ranks set by hand

The rank is the frequency of the spelling, not of the verb. Where the spelling is mostly a noun plural, or where wordfreq gave the verb row no score of its own, select.py sets the rank by hand. Each override moves the verb to another band; check-verb-cards.py fails one that does not. The ranks for noun plurals are judgement, not a lemma count. A second sense shares the rank of its base verb, because the frequency list counts spellings and cannot separate senses; only one row of a pair is listed here.

B1 completed and B2 added (20 November 2026)

The cap of sixty regulars was lifted for B1 and B2: every usable verb in those bands is now on a page, as a card or as a SKIP row with its reason. B1 now has 69 cards and B2 71. The budget for this pass was up to 120 new cards; the pass added 83 (27 in B1, 56 in B2). The review fix pass the same day added 11 more (verbs wrongly skipped for "two senses" or "another family drew it", plus gieten and wenken) and moved schaatsen back to B1. A third pass replaced every remaining "two senses" reason: ruimen, schakelen, pikken, isoleren and stoken were carded in their most drawable sense, and the other twenty kept SKIP with a reason about the picture. The B1/B2 pages stop short because 658 B1 and B2 verbs have no honest single drawing. Most of the B1/B2 tail is abstract, administrative, a screen action, a kind of speaking, or the same picture as a card that already exists. 2 drawable verbs wait for a later pass, each with its reason on the backlog.

What is not here

Separable verbs, reflexive verbs, a handful of sensitive or rare lemmas, the regular verbs below the A2 cut, and a few drawable B1/B2 verbs left for a later pass: these 530 verbs were held back before selection and are listed with their reason on backlog.html. The 780 skipped verbs were selected but have no honest single drawing; they are listed there too, under "Verbs the writer could not draw". The same page also tracks the other word classes still to be given cards.

Where the words came from

tools/_ww_tmp/select.py writes the selection; tools/build-verb-cards.py turns it, plus the scene notes in tools/_ww_tmp/scenes/, into these pages. Conventions and the rerun recipe are in AGENTS.html.
