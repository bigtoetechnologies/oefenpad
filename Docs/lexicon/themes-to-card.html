Words still to teach, grouped by theme

The master words that have no card yet, regrouped by semantic domain instead of by CEFR level.

Docs/lexicon/themes-to-card.html

On this page
  • The answer in one paragraph
  • Definitions and caveats
  • The themes in numbers
  • The most frequent words per theme
  • How to teach these

The answer in one paragraph

9,194 of the 10,000 master words have no card, and about 5,681 of those are plausible candidates for picture cards. The themes are the 29 semantic domains already tagged in tools/lexicon/woorden-domein.tsv; nothing was re-tagged by hand. The five themes with the most imageable uncarded words are Arts & media, Animals, Nature & weather, Work & jobs and Body & health. The full list is tools/lexicon/themes-to-card.tsv, rebuilt by tools/build-themes-page.py.

Definitions and caveats

Carded means the headword appears as a card in Werkwoorden, Bijvoeglijke naamwoorden, Zelfstandige naamwoorden, Bijwoorden, Scheidbare werkwoorden or Vaste uitdrukkingen. Sense ids and plural cards map to their lemma; a sense key such as bank (zitplaats) counts when its base word is carded in a family of the same part of speech. Voorzetselpaden is not counted, and prepositions are function words, so they fall outside picture cards anyway.

The domain tags are noisy. A lemma is filed under its most common nominal sense, so a verb or adjective can sit in a theme that fits only its noun. The part-of-speech tags are noisy too, and a few lemmas are tagged phrase or fall under the wrong class. Treat every count as a starting point for the owner, not a measurement.

Imageable estimate. The themes Abstract & general, Function words and Names, and the parts of speech function, name and interjection, count as not imageable. Everything else counts as an imageable candidate. This is a rough rule: many words in the remaining themes are still abstract or specialist.

How to teach these

The owner has decided four things for this group.

DecisionMeaning
Image-based cardsThe picture carries the meaning.
No example sentencesA card shows the word and the picture only.
Contrast pairs only where obviousOpposites are paired when the list itself shows them; no pair is forced.
No language-level bandsThis group is organised by theme, not by A0 to B2.

Three questions are still open and are not answered here.

  1. Card format: what a card for this group contains and how it is laid out.
  2. Who draws: who produces the artwork for the chosen words.
  3. Order of themes: which theme comes first and which words within it.
