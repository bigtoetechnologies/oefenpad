ook · alleen (enkel)

who has the thing · 2 cards

Alleen Eva heeft een ballon, maar Tom wil ook een ballon.Only Eva has a balloon, but Tom wants a balloon too.

Fixed frame. Two children of the same height stand side by side, whole-figure, facing the viewer, on a flat ground line with a plain wall behind. Each has one hand raised at shoulder height. Only what the children hold changes: a plain balloon on a string in the hands.

#1
geen beeld

who has the thingA0

Scene
Two children of the same height stand side by side, whole-figure, facing the viewer, and each holds one plain round balloon on a string in the raised hand, the two balloons floating at equal height above their heads. The wall behind is plain.
Must read
both children holding a balloon at equal height.
Avoid
one child holding a balloon and the other with empty hands (alleen-enkel); two children holding two different objects (not the pair); one child holding two balloons (veel); a single child (not the frame).
Contrast
Pair with alleen-enkel. The only difference is the second child's hand: holding a balloon here, empty there.
Example
Ik wil ook een kopje koffie.
I want a cup of coffee too.
Alt text
Twee kinderen staan naast elkaar en houden elk een ballon boven hun hoofd.
