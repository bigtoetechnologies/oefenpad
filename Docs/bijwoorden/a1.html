Where things are and which way: used up and left over, upstairs and downstairs, left and right, everywhere and nowhere, in and out, more and fewer, day and night.

op (leeg) · over (rest)

used up or left over · 2 cards

De koekjes zijn op, maar er is nog brood over.The cookies are finished, but there is still bread left.

Fixed frame. One plain glass jar with its lid off stands on a plain table beside one small plain plate, seen from the front, a plain wall behind, same sizes on the page. Only the cookies change: none left anywhere, or a few left.

#13
geen beeld

op

used up

used up or left overA1

Scene
The plain glass jar with its lid off stands upright and empty on the plain table, nothing inside it but one crumb, and the small plain plate beside it has two crumbs and no cookies. The wall behind is plain.
Must read
an empty upright jar and a plate with only crumbs.
Avoid
a plate with three cookies (over-rest); a jar full of cookies (vol); a jar with its lid on (dicht); a hungry face (not the card).
Contrast
Pair with over-rest. The only difference is the cookies: none, only crumbs, here; three still on the plate there.
Example
De melk is op, ik haal nieuwe.
The milk is finished, I will get new.
Alt text
Een lege pot staat naast een bord met alleen kruimels.

#23
geen beeld

meer

more

more or fewer than a referenceA1

Scene
The left plate holds four whole round apples as always; the right plate holds eight apples in two tidy rows, clearly twice as many. Both plates are the same size, the table and the wall plain.

the right plate visibly fuller, with twice the apples of the left.
