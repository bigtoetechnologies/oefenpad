place on a staircase · 3 cards

Mijn zoon staat bovenaan, mijn dochter onderaan en ik sta halverwege.My son is standing at the top, my daughter at the bottom and I am standing halfway.

Fixed frame. A plain staircase of ten steps with a plain handrail, seen from the side and rising to the right, with a flat landing at the top and a flat floor at the bottom. One adult in plain clothes, same size in every drawing. Only the step the adult stands on changes.

#47
geen beeld

bovenaan

at the top

place on a staircaseB1

Scene
The plain ten-step staircase rises to the right and the adult stands on the top step at the edge of the flat landing, one hand on the handrail, looking down the steps. The bottom of the stairs and the floor are empty.
Must read
the person on the topmost step at the landing, looking down the stairs.
Avoid
the person on the bottom step (onderaan); the person on the fifth step, halfway up (halverwege); the person on an upper floor with no staircase shown (boven); the person on the roof (buiten).
Contrast
Pair with onderaan and halverwege. The only difference is the step: the topmost step at the landing here, the bottom step or the fifth step there.
Example
Hij wacht bovenaan.
He is waiting at the top.
Alt text
Een man staat op de hoogste trede met een hand op de leuning en kijkt de trap af.
