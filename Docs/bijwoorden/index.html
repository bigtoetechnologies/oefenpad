How the cards work

Adverbs are taught in contrast sets: two or three words on one axis (hier/daar, binnen/buiten, rechtdoor/linksaf/rechtsaf). Every drawing in a set is the same picture with one visible thing changed, so the learner sees the pair, not the word. This family has no time adverbs, because one drawing per card cannot show before and after (see the backlog below).

A card carries the adverb, an English gloss, the scene brief, the one mark that must read, the near misses, the contrast note naming its partner and the single point of difference, an example sentence, and the alt text. Each set also carries a fixed frame and one Dutch sentence using every member.

How the sets were chosen

The sets were picked by hand from the lexicon words tagged adv, plus core adverbs and fixed phrases the tagger misses (rechtsaf, naar binnen, 's nachts, bij elkaar). Only pairs whose contrast is spatial or situational were kept. Manner adverbs that are really adjectives (snel, langzaam, hard, zacht) are left to Bijvoeglijke naamwoorden, and place words that are prepositions (op, in, naast) to Voorzetselpaden. Where an adverb shares a form with an adjective (alleen, af, bezig, mis, apart), the card draws the adverb sense only. The amount words (veel, weinig, genoeg, te veel, meer, minder, evenveel) are included as indefinite numerals: they are drawn and exemplified before a noun (veel appels, genoeg kopjes), not as adverbs in the strict sense, and stay here until a quantity family exists.

How a set was placed

A set sits in the band of its most frequent member (frequency rank in tools/lexicon/woorden-frequentie.tsv: A0 up to 250, A1 251–1000, A2 1001–2500, B1 2501–10000): the learner already knows one half and the card teaches its partner. Lexicon entries with a part-of-speech suffix (morgen (adv), al (adv), meer num, midden (adv)) are found by stripping the suffix; their ranks (8500 and up) look like placeholders, but none of them decides a band. 12 sets are placed by hand in OVERRIDE in tools/_bw_tmp/select.py, each for the reason below. 4 of them (vooraan-achteraan, verderop-hiernaast, dwars-schuin, boord) are everyday promotions under the owner rule that everyday items sit at A2 at the highest: their reason is rendered from the lexicon (niveau, top2000 and herkomst from woorden.tsv, and the rank), and Basis: judgment, not lexicon data says plainly that the lexicon does not support the move; the note gives the judgment. The checker re-reads every cited fact. Niveau open means in Bijlage 8 (a frequency list) but taught in neither coursebook.

Why this family has no B2 band

There are too few honest spatial adverbs past rank 5000 to fill a band, so B1 takes every set ranked 2501–10000. The expansion of 20 November 2026 aimed at ten new sets each in B1 and B2; six candidate B2 sets were written, three of them repeated another family's card and were dropped, and the three that remained are folded into B1. Most adverbs ranked 2501–10000 are time, frequency, degree or sentence adverbs (spoedig, dikwijls, enigszins, weliswaar, kortom, desnoods), which have no single picture. The spatial and situational candidates that remained were left out for the reasons below.

Left out on purpose

  • Time adverbs (gisteren, vandaag, morgen, nu, straks, altijd, nooit, vaak, zelden, soms, al, nog, eerst, daarna): a card has one drawing, a clock-free cue and no second panel, so before and after cannot be shown. Only the day-and-night pair (overdag, 's nachts) survives, drawn with sun and moon.
  • Stance adverbs (misschien, zeker, wel, niet): no object to draw, and a shrug, a tick or a cross needs text or is not in the style.
  • Sets that would redraw another family's card (hier/daar, binnen/buiten, samen/alleen, helemaal/bijna/een beetje, expres/per ongeluk): one drawing per word, so the other family keeps it. The backlog names the card each one duplicates.
  • Manner adverbs that duplicate adjective sets (snel, langzaam, hard, zacht, stil, luid, dichtbij, ver).
