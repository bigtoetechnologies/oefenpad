#1
geen beeld

op tijd

on time

arriving relative to a departureA0neutralliteral

Scene
an adult with a small bag stands calmly beside a plain bus-stop post, side view, whole figure, while a plain bus pulls in and stops next to the post with its front door open. The adult got there first and is not hurrying; the road line is flat and the sky empty.
Must read
the calm adult at the post beside the open-doored bus.
Avoid
an adult running after a bus pulling away (te laat); a bus driving past without stopping (not the card); an adult waiting alone on an empty road with no bus anywhere (not this set); a clock face (no text or numbers in the art).
Contrast
Pair with te-laat. The only difference is the timing: the bus pulls in beside a calm adult here, and drives away from a running adult there.
Example
Ik ben altijd op tijd.
I am always on time.
Alt text
Een vrouw met een tas staat rustig bij de halte terwijl de bus met open deur aankomt.

te laat

too late

arriving relative to a departureA0neutralliteral

Scene
an adult with a small bag runs along the flat road line after a plain bus whose back end is already several metres from the stop and moving off, one arm stretched out toward it, mouth open. The bus-stop post stands empty beside the road, the sky is empty.
Must read
a runner reaching toward a bus that is already leaving.
Avoid
an adult standing calmly as a bus arrives (op tijd); a person jogging for exercise with no bus in view (not the card); a bus parked at the stop with its door open (op tijd); an adult waving at a bus on a far road (not the card).
Contrast
Pair with op-tijd. The only difference is the timing: the bus drives away from a running adult here, and pulls in beside a calm adult there.
Example
Hij komt te laat voor de bus.
He is too late for the bus.
Alt text
Een man rent met een tas achter een bus aan die wegrijdt.

Fixed frame. the front wall of a plain building is cut away, and one adult in plain clothes sits whole-figure, side view, at a desk on a flat floor line, same adult and same size on the page. Only the room behind the cut-away wall changes: a classroom or an office.

Scene
the cut-away wall shows a classroom: the adult sits at a small pupil's desk in the second of three rows of identical desks, facing a blank board on the wall with nothing on it, a pen in the hand and a blank open exercise book on the desk.
Must read
an adult at a pupil's desk among rows of desks, facing a blank board.
Avoid

Contrast
Pair with op-het-werk. The only difference is the room: a pupil's desk among rows of desks facing a blank board here, a single office desk with a blank screen there.
Example

Een vrouw zit aan een tafeltje tussen rijen tafeltjes en kijkt naar een leeg bord.

met de auto · met de bus · met de trein

which road vehicle · 3 cards

Wij gaan met de auto, jullie gaan met de bus en zij gaan met de trein.We go by car, you go by bus and they go by train.

Fixed frame. one adult in plain clothes sits inside a moving road vehicle and is seen through its side window, the whole vehicle in side view on a flat road line, three short speed lines behind it, the vehicle filling the width of the picture. Only the kind of vehicle changes.

#5
geen beeld

met de auto

by car

which road vehicleA0neutralliteral

Scene
the adult sits at the wheel of a plain small family car and is seen through the front side window, both hands on the steering wheel, the car driving along the road line with two wheels visible and three speed lines behind it. No other vehicle is in the picture.
Must read
an adult at the wheel of a small car with speed lines.
Avoid
an adult seated by a window in a long vehicle with a row of windows (met de bus); an adult seated in a carriage on rails (met de trein); an adult loading a suitcase into a boot (not the card); a lorry (not the card).
Contrast
Pair with met-de-bus and met-de-trein. The only difference is the vehicle: a small car with the adult at the wheel here, a long road bus or a carriage on rails there.
Example
Wij gaan met de auto naar zee.
We go to the sea by car.
Alt text
Een man zit achter het stuur van een kleine personenwagen die over de weg rijdt.
