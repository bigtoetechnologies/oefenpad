op de planken · in de coulissen

where the actress stands · 2 cards

Eerst wacht de actrice in de coulissen, dan staat zij op de planken.First the actress waits in the wings, then she is on stage.

Fixed frame. a small theatre stage seen from the audience: a plain wooden stage floor, a plain curtain gathered at each side and one tall plain side flat at the right edge, the backs of three rows of seats in the foreground; one actress in a long plain coat; same stage, same size on the page. Only where the actress stands changes.

#91
geen beeld

op de planken

on the stage

where the actress standsB2neutralliteral

Scene
the actress stands in the middle of the stage floor facing the seats, one arm stretched out and her mouth open as if speaking; short rays from one lamp above fall on her. Behind the side flat at the right edge nobody is visible.
Must read
the actress in the middle of the stage, facing the seats.
Avoid
the actress half hidden behind the side flat (in de coulissen); an audience clapping (not the card); a singer at a microphone on a podium (not the card); a teacher in front of a class (not the card).
Contrast
Pair with in-de-coulissen. The only difference is where the actress stands: in the middle of the stage facing the seats here, half hidden behind the side flat there.
Example
Mijn oma stond vroeger op de planken.
My grandmother used to be an actress.
Alt text
Een actrice in een lange jas staat midden op een toneelvloer voor rijen stoelen en spreekt met een uitgestrekte arm.

in de coulissen

in the wings

where the actress standsB2neutralliteral and figurative

Scene
the actress stands at the side behind the tall flat at the right edge, only half visible: one shoulder, one eye and one hand on the edge of the flat, peeking at the stage. The middle of the stage floor is empty and lit by short rays from one lamp above.
Must read
the actress half hidden behind the side flat, the stage empty.
Avoid
the actress in the middle of the stage facing the seats (op de planken); a person hiding behind a curtain at home (not the card); a person at a mirror in a dressing room (not the card); a stagehand pulling a rope (not the card).
Contrast
Pair with op-de-planken. The only difference is where the actress stands: half hidden behind the side flat here, in the middle of the stage facing the seats there.
Example
De zanger wacht zenuwachtig in de coulissen.
The singer waits nervously in the wings.
Alt text
Een actrice in een lange jas gluurt half verstopt achter een hoog zijscherm naar een leeg, verlicht toneel.

in draf · in galop

how fast the horse moves · 2 cards

Het paard loopt eerst in draf en gaat daarna in galop.The horse first trots and then breaks into a gallop.

Fixed frame. one plain horse without saddle or rider on a flat sandy track, side view, moving to the right, a low wooden fence behind; same horse, same track, same size on the page. Only the horse's legs and body change.

#93
geen beeld

in draf

at a trot

how fast the horse movesB2neutralliteral

Scene
the horse trots: the front leg on one side and the back leg on the other side are lifted and bent together, while the other two legs stand on the sand; the head is up, the neck arched, the tail hangs and one small puff of sand rises.
Must read
two diagonal legs lifted together and two on the ground.
Avoid
all four legs stretched out and the body low (in galop); a horse standing on fenced grass (in de wei); a horse with a rider (te paard); a horse jumping a fence (not the card).
Contrast
Pair with in-galop. The only difference is the stride: diagonal legs lifted in pairs with the head up here, all legs stretched out with the body low there.
Example
De pony komt in draf naar het hek.
The pony comes trotting to the gate.
Alt text
Een paard loopt over zand en tilt telkens een voorbeen en het schuin tegenovergestelde achterbeen tegelijk op.

in galop

at a gallop

how fast the horse movesB2neutralliteral

Scene
the horse gallops: its body is stretched long and low, both front legs reach far forward and both back legs push far back, all four hooves are off the sand, the mane and tail fly out behind and three puffs of sand rise.
Must read
a stretched low body with all four hooves off the ground.
Avoid
diagonal legs lifted in pairs with the head up (in draf); a horse rearing on its back legs (not the card); a racehorse with a rider and a number cloth (not the card); a horse's head over a stable door (op stal).
Contrast
Pair with in-draf. The only difference is the stride: all legs stretched out with the body low here, diagonal legs lifted in pairs with the head up there.
Example
De paarden gingen in galop over het strand.
The horses galloped along the beach.
Alt text
Een paard vliegt lang en laag gestrekt over het zand, met alle vier de hoeven van de grond en wapperende manen.

in de pas · uit de pas

walking in step or out of it · 2 cards

Eerst loopt het kind in de pas, daarna loopt het uit de pas.First the child walks in step, then it walks out of step.

Fixed frame. four children in the same plain coats walking toward the viewer side by side in one wide row on a plain path, front view, each with one knee raised and the opposite arm swinging forward; same children, same path, same size on the page. Only which knee the third child from the left raises changes.

#95
geen beeld

in de pas

in step

walking in step or out of itB2neutralliteral and figurative

Scene
all four children raise the knee on the viewer's left at the same moment, bent to the same height, the other foot flat on the path and the opposite arm swinging forward, so the four bodies look like copies of one another. Nobody stumbles or turns round.
Must read
four children abreast, each with the same knee raised.
Avoid
the third child raising the other knee (uit de pas); four children in one line one behind the other (in ganzenpas); soldiers marching with rifles (not the card); children standing still side by side in a row (naast elkaar).
Contrast
Pair with uit-de-pas. The only difference is the third child's raised knee: the same knee as the other three here, the other knee there.
Example
De muzikanten lopen netjes in de pas.
The musicians walk neatly in step.
Alt text
Vier kinderen lopen in één brede rij naar je toe en tillen allemaal tegelijk dezelfde knie op.

#96
geen beeld

uit de pas

out of step

walking in step or out of itB2neutralliteral and figurative

Scene
three children raise the knee on the viewer's left, but the third child from the left raises the other knee and swings the other arm forward, so its body is the mirror image of the other three. All four walk toward the viewer, and nobody stumbles or turns round.
Must read
one child with the opposite knee raised to the other three.
Avoid
all four children raising the same knee (in de pas); a child tripping and falling (not the card); a child walking the other way (not the card); a child standing still with both feet down (not the card).
Contrast
Pair with in-de-pas. The only difference is the third child's raised knee: the other knee here, the same knee as the other three there.
Example
Hij loopt altijd een beetje uit de pas.
He is always a bit out of step.
Alt text
Vier kinderen lopen in één brede rij naar je toe; drie tillen dezelfde knie op, het derde kind juist de andere.

in ganzenpas · op een kluitje

how the children walk together · 2 cards

Eerst lopen de kinderen in ganzenpas, daarna lopen ze op een kluitje.First the children walk in single file, then they walk bunched together.

Fixed frame. five children in the same plain coats walking to the right along a narrow path through a plain field, seen from slightly above; same children, same path, same size on the page. Only how the five walk together changes.

#97
geen beeld

in ganzenpas

in single file

how the children walk togetherB2neutralliteral

Scene
the five children walk one behind the other in one straight line along the middle of the path, an equal small gap between each, all facing forward; nobody walks beside anybody else.
Must read
five children in one straight line, one behind the other.
Avoid
the five children pressed close together in one small group (op een kluitje); identical cups standing one behind the other (achter elkaar); soldiers marching with stiff straight legs (not the card); children holding hands in a circle (not the card); a queue at a counter (not the card).
Contrast
Pair with op-een-kluitje. The only difference is how the children walk: one behind the other in a single line here, pressed close together in one group there.
Example
De eendjes zwemmen in ganzenpas achter hun moeder.
The ducklings swim in single file behind their mother.
Alt text
Vijf kinderen lopen op een pad in één rechte lijn, de een vlak na de ander.

op een kluitje

huddled together

how the children walk togetherB2neutralliteral

Scene
the five children walk pressed tightly together in one small group in the middle of the path, shoulders touching, some half hidden behind others, all facing forward; nobody walks alone in front or behind.
Must read
five children pressed close together in one small group.
Avoid
the five children in one straight line, one behind the other (in ganzenpas); children standing in a neat row side by side (naast elkaar); a crowd at a concert (not the card); children hugging (not the card); blocks pushed together in one cluster (bij elkaar, Bijwoorden).
Contrast
Pair with in-ganzenpas. The only difference is how the children walk: pressed close together in one group here, one behind the other in a single line there.
Example
De schapen staan op een kluitje bij het hek.
The sheep stand huddled together by the gate.
Alt text
Vijf kinderen lopen dicht tegen elkaar aan in één klein groepje midden op een pad.
