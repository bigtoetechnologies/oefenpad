#8
geen beeld

te voet

on foot

how a person travels by body or animalA1neutralliteral

Scene
the adult walks along the path, one foot lifted behind and the other flat on the ground, arms swinging, backpack straps visible, no vehicle and no animal anywhere in the picture. Small stones lie on the path beside the feet.
Must read
two feet walking, with no vehicle or animal.
Avoid
an adult on a bicycle (op de fiets); an adult on a horse (te paard); a runner in sports clothes (not the card); an adult being carried (not the card).
Contrast
