Explanation cards

Verbs with no single honest drawing, taught with a picture, a short Dutch scene and one yes/no question, one card per sense.

Docs/explanation-cards/index.html

State

926 cards for 784 verbs, 3704 examples, as briefs and static mocks. No drawings exist.

Rules

The rules are in Docs/decisions.html and the format is shown in the pilot. In short: a picture, two to four short Dutch sentences, one ja/nee question, four examples per sense, every word at or below the card's band, senses from Dutch Wiktionary (nl.wiktionary.org).

Rebuild

Edit the JSON files in tools/_ex_tmp/, then run python3 tools/build-explanation-cards.py. The build fails on any card that breaks a rule. Rerunning overwrites these pages.

Limits

  • The Dutch and alt text are AI-written; no native speaker has read them.
  • Examples 2 to 4 carry a scene line only; alt text is written when they are drawn.
  • Where a surface form has several homograph keys, the best rank is used, so the check is generous there.

On hold

VerbSenseWhy held
voorzienuitrusten met iets (voorzien van, to provide with)formal register at A1; reviewer recommends B1 or dropping
bestrijdende strijd aanbinden met iets (to fight, to combat)natural objects (ongedierte, onkruid, muggen) sit above A2; examples 2 and 4 repeat each other
voegenzich bij iemand of iets aansluiten (to join; zich voegen bij)the only Wiktionary senses (legal, masonry) are above A2; the everyday zich voegen bij is formal and is rarely used at A2
plegengewend zijn te doen, vaak doen (plegen te + infinitief) (to be in the habit of)formal and old-fashioned: plegen te + infinitive is written Dutch; a learner at A2 says meestal or altijd instead, so the card would teach a stiff pattern
combinerentwee dingen door elkaar doen (to mix)Dutch speakers say mengen for mixing paint or juice; combineren teaches an unnatural use. The other combineren card covers the verb.
zendensturen (to send, formal)zenden is formal or old-fashioned as a plain verb; everyday Dutch uses sturen. Natural examples only exist in compounds (verzenden, uitzenden) or stiff style, so these four are stiff and a card would teach register more than sense.
wijkenopzij gaan voor iets of iemand (to give way)'wijken voor' is formal and dated; the natural phrases need words above the ceiling. Lexicon rank is a homograph artefact of wijk.
overwinneneen tegenstander verslaan (to defeat an opponent)In sport Dutch speakers say verslaan; overwinnen an opponent sounds stilted. The overcome-a-weakness card covers the verb.
wakenopzettelijk wakker blijven (to stay awake on purpose)Stiff: Dutch says opblijven for staying awake. The waken-over card covers the verb.
bevriezeniets door afkoeling vast laten worden (to freeze, tr.)Dutch freezes food with invriezen; bevriezen something sounds off. The intransitive card covers the verb.
barenjongen op de wereld brengen (of an animal: to give birth)Unnatural with animals (Dutch says jongen krijgen/werpen). The natural uses are zorgen baren and opzien baren: needs a new card.
bestempeleneen stempel zetten op iets (to stamp)Present-day Dutch says afstempelen for putting a stamp on paper; the label-as card covers the verb.
verzinkenin iets wegzakken (to sink into)Dutch speakers say wegzakken or zinken for sinking into mud; only the lost-in-thought card sounds natural.
bezienin ogenschouw nemen (to view, to consider)Only natural in fixed phrases (van dichtbij bezien, zo bezien) or formal register; plain Dutch uses bekijken.
tuttenlangdurig met schoonheidsverzorging bezig zijn (to fuss over one's looks)Speakers say zich optutten; bare tutten for grooming is marginal. The dawdle card covers the verb.
offereniets waardevols opgeven voor iets anders (to sacrifice)Bare offeren sounds religious or high-flown; the everyday verb for giving something up is opofferen. Needs a decision on how to card this verb.
linkenbedriegen (informal: to trick, to cheat someone)Informal slang sense; most speakers read linken as connect. Needs a new card.
